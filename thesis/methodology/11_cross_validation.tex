\section{Cross-Validation}
\label{sec:method-cv}

The risk models were evaluated with $k$-fold cross-validation on the clean-baseline cohort instead of a fixed split into training, validation and test sets, so every participant contributed to both fitting and evaluation and no events were set aside in an unused test set.
The folds were formed per participant and, for each artery, stratified by new plaque in that trunk, so the proportion of events was the same in every fold.
By design, no independent test set was held out, and the reported performance is the mean over the held-out folds.
