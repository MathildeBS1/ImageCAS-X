\section{Week 2: 7-11. September 2026}

\sectionprogress

\textbf{Pipeline chapter written.} I walked through CAS-Net end to end -- data preparation,
network architecture, training, inference and evaluation metrics -- and wrote it up as a standalone
chapter (\Cref{ch:pipeline}). Doing this properly meant reading the actual training and inference
code, not just the paper: the resampling and cropping choices, the three attention modules (SAFE,
AGFF, MSFA), the Dice loss and Adam schedule, the tiled inference with mirror-flip TTA, and the full
metric family used to score it (Dice, HD95, Betti numbers, clDice, centerline distances).

\textbf{Scope narrowed.} Rather than the whole coronary tree, the topology/feature-extraction work
will focus for a start on three vessels -- the left main, LAD and LCX -- for the reasons laid out in
\Cref{sec:week2-major-vessel}. Segmentation, dominance annotation and centerline extraction still
need the whole tree; only the population-level feature analysis is being narrowed.

\textbf{State-of-the-art notes drafted.} Three standalone arguments moved out of the state-of-the-art
chapter and got expanded this week: the segmentation benchmark's topology gap (overlap looks fine,
Betti error does not, and this thesis targets post-hoc topological repair rather than retraining),
which feature is actually standardised across the field (bifurcation angle) versus contested
(tortuosity, where I plan to spend most of my method-development effort) -- see ``Features'' below --
and why the population CCTA is normally drawn from is the wrong one for questions about pre-disease
geometry, which is the argument for why the Copenhagen General Population Study
(Herlev--{\O}sterbro) matters here -- see ``Population'' below.

\subsection{Questions}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Is narrowing feature analysis to LM/LAD/LCX (\Cref{sec:week2-major-vessel}) the right call, or
    should RCA be included from the start given its own plaque burden?
  \item For the tortuosity work: is there an existing 3D-aware metric in the literature I should
    benchmark against before designing a new one, or is the field genuinely starting from the index
    formula and 2D shape classes described in the ``Features'' note below?
  \item Phillip -- on the CGPS cohort definition (``Population'' note below): should participants
    with known CAD be excluded from the "healthy" baseline, and is the Herlev--{\O}sterbro naming the
    study's own preferred wording, or just how it is known informally?
\end{itemize}
