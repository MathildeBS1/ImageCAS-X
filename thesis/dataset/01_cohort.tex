\section{Cohort and image quality}
\label{sec:dataset-cohort}

ImageCAS-X annotates coronary centerlines, segmentation labels and a descriptor table over the
ImageCAS cohort of CCTA scans \cite{zeng2023imagecas,bransby2026imagecasx}.
Image quality is graded from 0 to 4, and the 200 cases graded 0 are excluded from the released
cohort, leaving the 800 usable cases used throughout this thesis \cite{bransby2026imagecasx}.

Among those 800 the grading remains informative rather than uniform.
Because a lower-quality scan plausibly yields fewer traceable distal branches, image quality is a
candidate confounder for any branch-count statistic derived from this cohort.
