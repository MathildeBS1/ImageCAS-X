\section{Imaging parameters}
\label{sec:dataset-imaging}

The ImageCAS-X volumes have in-plane spacing between 0.318 mm and 0.43 mm and a slice thickness
of 0.5 mm, varying between cases \cite{zeng2023imagecas}.
This bounds every geometric measurement in this thesis (Section~\ref{sec:ccta}): a vessel two or
three voxels across cannot support a confident radius measurement, which places a floor on the
branch order at which geometric features remain meaningful.

Contrast opacification in this cohort is adequate: sampling the volumes at the annotated
centerline points, after converting them from the centerlines' LPS frame into voxel indices,
gives a median of 336 HU, consistent with a contrast-filled lumen.

Neither ImageCAS-X nor the ImageCAS cohort beneath it records pressure inside the artery
\cite{bransby2026imagecasx,zeng2023imagecas}, so FFR (Section~\ref{sec:ffr}) cannot be computed
from these images; wall shear stress, which follows from the vessel geometry alone, is the
hemodynamic quantity this dataset can support.
