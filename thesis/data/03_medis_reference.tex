\section{Medis Centerlines and Contours}
\label{sec:data-medis}

Two further data sets were derived in Medis from different selections of scans.
The first holds centerline trees for 744 scans, stored as polylines in which every point carries the label of its coronary segment.
The LAD, LCx and RCA trunks were complete in 576, 581 and 553 of them.
The second holds cross-sectional contours of the lumen and of the plaque, with the lumen and plaque volumes, for the main vessels with plaque in [n] scans.
Both data sets were used as the reference for the centerlines and lumen from the in-house tracer (Sections~\ref{sec:method-tracer} and~\ref{sec:method-medis}).
They cover only selected scans, and the contours only vessels with plaque, so they are not drawn from the clean-baseline cohort, and they were treated as a reference rather than as ground truth.
