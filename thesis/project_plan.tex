% Week 4 implementation note, deliberately kept out of the table until it is
% understood well enough to defend. The ImageCAS-X paper documents how the
% delivered centerlines were actually made, and it changes this row.
%
% They are not hand-traced. Analysts traced and refined centerlines (200 h),
% those drove the cMPR lumen annotation, the mask was U-Net predicted and then
% manually corrected slice by slice (270 h) -- and then NEW centerlines were
% regenerated from the corrected masks by skeletonization (Lee, Kashyap & Chu
% 1994, i.e. skimage's method='lee') followed by Gaussian smoothing
% (sigma = 0.5 mm over a 5-vertex sliding window). Those are what ships.
%
% Two consequences. First, the recipe is known, so it gives an exact target:
% skeletonizing the GROUND-TRUTH mask with the same algorithm should reproduce
% the delivered centerline closely, which is a far sharper check than "looks
% right". Second, the reference is a skeleton of an expert-corrected mask, so
% the human effort sits in the masks and the segment names, not in the
% centerline geometry -- agreement with it bounds correctness rather than
% proving it.
%
% Hence two extractors, built and compared rather than one chosen blind:
%   (a) the matching recipe -- skeletonize + smooth exactly as above;
%   (b) VMTK mesh-based, which returns branches, branch points and a radius
%       per point as maximum inscribed sphere.
% Comparing them is what lets a disagreement be attributed to the extractor
% instead of being confounded with it.
%
% Korona & Baldachowski (Former_students_work/) are reference [26] of the
% ImageCAS-X paper: their tracer is what initialized these centerlines. Their
% section 5.1 used VMTK on ImageCAS segmentations and documents its weak spot
% -- it loses accuracy where vessels narrow, so they dilated the mask there and
% accepted overestimated radii. That is a reason to compare, not to assume.
%
% Calibre is new work either way: the delivered centerlines carry no radius
% array at all (only segment_label, segment_name, start/branch/end_points), so
% radius has to come from a distance transform on the mask.
%
% The row stays a 4, but for different reasons than before: two extractors plus
% a skeleton-to-graph step that does not exist anywhere in the codebase yet,
% and VMTK still not installed in the venv.

\caption{Project plan over the 16 available weeks, with the risk of delay from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
The two cohorts play different roles.
ImageCAS-X is used where ground truth is needed --- reference centerlines, per-artery labels and an
independent dominance annotation over 800 cases --- so the methods of objectives 5--7 are developed
and validated there, and it remains the reference distribution afterwards.
Those reference centerlines are an algorithmic derivative of expert-corrected masks rather than a
hand-drawn ground truth, so agreement with them bounds correctness rather than establishing it.
The expert effort they carry is in the lumen masks and the segment names.
Branch-level features inherit the annotation protocol as well: side branches below 1~mm were not
traced, and septal perforators, acute marginals and nodal arteries were not traced at all, so a
branch count describes the protocol as much as the anatomy and has to be reported as such.
The Rigshospitalet cohort carries objectives 8--10: the population description, the outlier
detection and the association with outcome. What it contains is not yet known, so the plan commits
only to methods that do not depend on it having expert centerlines, artery labels or a particular
outcome variable.
The schedule spends about a week per objective because objective 6 is already built and validated
and objective 5 is reproduction from delivered weights rather than training. That is what allows the
move to the second cohort in week~6, and what leaves week~14 open.
The delivered weights were trained on this dataset's own 560-case training split, so every number
that compares a predicted tree against the ground truth is quoted on the 160 test cases, and any
fine-tune stays on the training split.
Nothing before week~6 depends on the external data, and if it arrives late or thin, weeks 8--12
fall back to work that needs no second cohort: per-segment and per-territory analysis, the hemodynamic
surrogates, and a deeper study of how far the features survive automatic segmentation.
Weeks 12--13 are what turn description into prediction. Wall shear stress is the mechanism linking
the shape of the tree to where plaque forms, so week~12 gives a functional endpoint that does not
depend on how rich the outcome data proves to be, and week~13 asks whether those features predict
risk beyond the clinical variables already available.}
