# Coronary geometry, WSS and geometry measurement: papers from 2023 to 2026 missing from the literature review

Search date 2026-11-08. Sources: OpenAlex API (title/abstract search filtered to 2023 onward and sorted by citations, plus per-DOI counts with `counts_by_year`), the Semantic Scholar batch API (one batch, then rate-limited with HTTP 429), Europe PMC abstracts and web search. About 20 tool calls. Google Scholar could not be queried. It usually runs about 1.3 to 2 times higher than OpenAlex (OA) or Semantic Scholar (S2), so read the counts as a ranking. "Cit/yr" is the OA total divided by the years since online publication, with OA's count per calendar year in brackets. Verdicts rest on abstracts, not full texts, unless stated otherwise. CAS-Net and `shen2026geometry` were not searched for or considered.

Recent work already in `refs.bib` and cited: `kashyap2022tortuosity`, `zhang2024curvature` (RSOS 2024; OA 13), `garcha2025sensitivity` (OA 12), `griffo2026wss`, `telloayala2026tortuosity`, `schultz2023ess`, `nannini2024characterization` (APL Bioeng 2024; OA 44, the most cited 2023–2026 coronary-geometry measurement paper found), `zebicmihic2024index`, `wang2024lmphenotypes`, `tommasino2024clap`, `chen2024bridge`, `lee2024radiomics` and `taylor2024murray` (Murray's-law meta-analysis). The independent check agrees with the earlier reports: **no widely cited (more than about 50 citations) primary study from 2023–2026 on coronary geometry and WSS, or on geometry measurement, is missing.** The 2023–2026 papers with more than 100 citations are biology or narrative WSS reviews, or plaque/hemodynamics event studies (EMERALD II). One correction to earlier notes: Gharleghi et al. was filed as a preprint (arXiv 2401.12504). It has since been **published in Scientific Reports (2025)**, so the "preprint, never cite" rule no longer applies to it.

## Recent ESC/EAPCI/SCCT consensus or high-impact reviews on WSS, coronary geometry or shape as a risk marker (2023–2026)?

### Takeaway
No ESC, EAPCI or SCCT consensus on WSS or coronary geometry from 2023–2026 was found. `gijsen2019wss` (ESC Working Group, EHJ 2019) is still the latest expert consensus on coronary WSS. The highly cited recent reviews are about endothelial biology or are broad narrative reviews, and none holds a concept the thesis lacks.

### Cited Findings
- A web search for a 2024–2025 EHJ/ESC consensus on coronary WSS returned only the 2019 expert recommendations. The nearest newer item is a JAHA review, not a consensus — [search result: Ekmejian 2024 record](https://doaj.org/article/f60ba8c13a9f453ca414e16a9ee7ae2c); [2019 EHJ recommendations](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/153395)
- Ekmejian et al. 2024 (JAHA): "the clinical application of these techniques has been limited to hemodynamic assessment of coronary disease severity", and "Improved computational efficiency and large clinical trials are required"; OA 38 citations — [Europe PMC abstract, PMID 39291505](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/39291505); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.124.037129)
- Zhou et al. 2023 (Front Cardiovasc Med) is the most cited 2023+ WSS review (OA 258, S2 238) — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcvm.2023.1083547)
- Tamargo et al. 2023 (Nat Rev Cardiol) on flow-induced endothelial reprogramming: OA 306, S2 276 — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41569-023-00883-1)
- Wang et al. 2023 (Cardiovasc Res) on endothelial mechanobiology: OA 154, S2 143 — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/cvr/cvad076)
- The Quantitative Cardiovascular Imaging Study Group consensus (Nat Rev Cardiol 2023) covers stenosis and plaque quantification by imaging modality, not geometry: OA 137, S2 123 — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41569-023-00880-4)
- Candreva et al. 2024 meta-analysis: 6 studies, 136 patients, 31,385 segments. Lower WSS was associated with lumen reduction (MD −0.88), more plaque burden (MD 4.32) and more necrotic core at follow-up — [Europe PMC, PMID 38641263](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/38641263)
- Bacigalupi et al. 2024 (Front Cardiovasc Med), a review of biomechanical factors and where atherosclerosis localizes: OA 19 — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcvm.2024.1392702)

### Inferences
- The "no recent consensus" point helps the thesis. Gijsen 2019 stays the single authoritative WSS source, and nothing newer contradicts it.
- If an examiner asks for a recent source, Candreva 2024 is the only one that adds a quantitative evidence synthesis (low WSS precedes progression). It is small and all intravascular, and under the one-source rule it would replace, not join, Chatzizisis 2007 or Stone 2012 for that concept.

### Gaps
- No full text of Ekmejian 2024 or Candreva 2024 was read. Their inclusion criteria and risk-of-bias assessments are unverified.
- SCCT and EAPCI document lists were not browsed directly. The absence of a consensus rests on web and OpenAlex searches.

## Recent CCTA-derived geometry or WSS studies with follow-up (CCTA-based ESS predicting progression or events, CArTI, deep-learning whole-tree tortuosity)?

### Takeaway
No 2023–2026 study relates CCTA-derived WSS or geometry at baseline to later plaque in plaque-free people. All follow-up studies with CCTA-based hemodynamics are in diseased or ACS patients and test lesions (EMERALD II, De Nisco 2024, Gu 2024). The only 2023–2026 study relating whole-artery shape to a later outcome is Griffo 2025, which used invasive 3D-QCA. CArTI is still an abstract.

### Cited Findings
- EMERALD II (Koo et al., JACC CVI 2024): 351 patients with ACS 1 month to 3 years after CCTA; 2,088 non-culprit and 363 culprit lesions. The best AI features were lesion FFR, plaque burden, total and low-attenuation plaque volume and myocardial blood flow. OA 115, S2 107 (2026-11-08) — [Europe PMC, PMID 38752951](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/38752951)
- De Nisco et al. 2024 (ATVB): 37 patients with ACS, CCTA plus IVUS/NIRS/OCT at baseline and 1 year. High TSVI and low TAWSS were associated with higher plaque progression — [Europe PMC, PMID 38328935](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/38328935)
- Hakim et al. 2023 (JCCT): local ESS from CCTA was similar to ESS from IVUS. A validation study, without outcome — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcct.2023.03.009)
- A web search found no 2024–2025 longitudinal CCTA-ESS outcome study. A 2025 JCCT study linked low-ESS area to 18F-NaF activity in ACS, cross-sectionally (snippet) — [search result page](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10017098/)
- Griffo et al. 2025 (J Biomech): PCA+LDA statistical shape model of 69 LADs from 3D-QCA separated future culprit lesions of MI at 5 years from non-culprit ones (p<0.0001), including after virtual removal of the main lesion — [Europe PMC, PMID 40561693](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/40561693)
- CArTI: an AHA 2025 conference abstract (OA type "conference-abstract", 1 citation). Two searches this session found no full paper — [AHA abstract](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/circ.152.suppl_3.4365988)
- Leng et al. (ESC 2026 abstract, APOLLO 848 patients): curvature-based tortuosity was associated with stenosis severity in each artery, and hypertension amplified it. Cross-sectional — [citedrive record](https://www.citedrive.com/en/discovery/coronary-artery-tortuosity-is-independently-associated-with-stenosis-severity-obstructive-disease-and-hypertension/); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehag785.175)
- Nannini et al. 2024 (APL Bioeng, already cited): a fully automated deep-learning pipeline (two-stage U-Net, 281 CCTA) for coronary segmentation, CAC and tortuosity score. Tortuosity correlated negatively with calcified plaque. OA 44 — [PMC10807932](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10807932/)
- Cobo et al. 2023 (Sci Rep): a CNN detected tortuosity on invasive angiograms as well as experts. OA/S2 4 — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-37868-6)

### Inferences
- The gap statement ("no study has related named geometric features measured in plaque-free persons to later plaque") still holds after this search.
- Griffo 2025 weakens "in the coronary arteries this has so far been shown in only one study" (end of `sec:lit-measure`) if "shape" is read broadly. The sentence stays correct only for the curvature profile.
- EMERALD II is the paper most likely to be named by a CCTA-oriented examiner. The reply is that it studies events in existing lesions, so it sits outside the question, the same reasoning the earlier report applied to Motoyama and ICONIC.

### Gaps
- Griffo 2025 full text was not read. The exact shape representation (centerline vs surface) and the cross-validation scheme are unknown.
- The EMERALD II page numbers were not verified. Whether WSS or axial plaque stress entered the candidate features is unclear from the abstract.
- CArTI cohort type and plaque status are still unknown.

## Recent studies of the left main bifurcation angle, diameter ratios or taper against CAD on CCTA?

### Takeaway
The 2023–2026 bifurcation-angle studies against CAD are small, cross-sectional and from referred patients (Czaja-Ziółkowska 2023, Moon 2023, Zhang 2023 on the ramus), and none needs adding. For diameter ratios, Gharleghi et al. 2025 (Sci Rep) is the one real-patient CTCA study in which Finet's ratio was the leading correlate of low WSS. It is the best candidate in this note. No 2023–2026 taper study beyond those in `reports/Recent lumen taper studies.md` was found.

### Cited Findings
- Gharleghi et al. 2025: in 127 LM bifurcations from CTCA of patients with suspected CAD and no significant stenosis, Finet's ratio correlated with low TAESS (p<0.001). Vessel diameters and curvature correlated with high OSI and mean TSVI. Finet's ratio, diameters and daughter-branch curvature correlated independently with RRT — [Europe PMC, PMID 39747107](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/39747107); OA 11, S2 11
- Gharleghi et al. 2025 (IEEE TBME), same 127 patients (42 men, 85 women): women had more inner-wall low TAESS (16.8% vs 10.7%), men more high RRT (6.1% vs 4.2%) and high OSI (4.6% vs 2.3%) — [Europe PMC, PMID 40031780](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/40031780)
- Czaja-Ziółkowska et al. 2023: 52 patients with LM trifurcation (of 266 who had CCTA). The angle was wider with a diseased LCA (108° vs 91°, p=0.04), and the "age at onset" was back-calculated by assuming 25% CAC growth per year — [Europe PMC, PMID 35775448](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/35775448)
- Moon et al. 2023 (QIMS): pLAD tortuosity combined with the LM–LAD angle predicted severe pLAD stenosis. Retrospective and cross-sectional — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.21037/qims-23-518)
- Zhang D-Q et al. 2023 (BMC Med Imaging): the ramus intermedius was not an independent risk factor for atherosclerosis in the LM bifurcation, but may raise pLAD risk — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12880-023-01009-2)
- Yu et al. 2025 (Clin Radiol): average torsion of the proximal and mid LAD was among the predictors of moderate-to-severe LAD stenosis in a 115-case training set — [Europe PMC, PMID 40043631](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/40043631)
- The OpenAlex search for "coronary taper" and "coronary lumen tapering" in 2023+ returned no coronary-geometry taper study (results were stent sizing and unrelated hits) — [OpenAlex API](https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=title_and_abstract.search:coronary%20lumen%20tapering,from_publication_date:2023-01-01)

### Inferences
- Gharleghi 2025 would let `sec:lit-wss` support "the division of diameter at the bifurcation" with real patients, not only Garcha's synthetic trees. Its patients have no significant stenosis, the closest any recent CFD study comes to the thesis's plaque-free cohort, though not plaque-free. It probably shares its patients with Kashyap 2022 (Beier group), so citing it as an independent replication would overstate it. It is a second feature analysis of the same data.
- The angle literature from 2023–2026 adds nothing to the existing angle sources. Its direction (wider angle with more disease) matches Tommasino, and its quality is lower.

### Gaps
- Whether Gharleghi 2025 and Kashyap 2022 use exactly the same 127 patients was not confirmed from full text.
- No 2023–2026 CCTA taper-versus-CAD study was found. This rests on OpenAlex title and abstract search, not on PubMed MeSH.

## Any 2023–2026 FPCA or shape-analysis study of coronary centerlines?

### Takeaway
No 2023–2026 FPCA study of coronary centerlines or curvature profiles was found. The nearest is Griffo et al. 2025, a multivariate PCA statistical shape model of whole LAD geometries against future MI in 69 invasive 3D-QCA reconstructions. It is shape analysis, not FPCA.

### Cited Findings
- An extended web search for FPCA of coronary centerline curvature (2024) found no such study. Its closest results were Medrano-Gracia's point-based SSM of normal bifurcations (pre-2023, already cited) and Griffo 2025 — [search result: Griffo 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021929025003410); [Medrano-Gracia, PMC5323506](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5323506)
- OpenAlex searches for "functional principal component coronary", "functional data analysis coronary" and "vessel centerline functional data" (2023+) returned no coronary FPCA geometry paper — [OpenAlex API](https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=title_and_abstract.search:functional%20principal%20component%20coronary,from_publication_date:2023-01-01)
- Ghorbannia and Randles 2024 (EMBC conference paper): automated alignment and landmarking of 73 coronary trees, a methods step towards shape analysis, OA 1 — [Europe PMC, PMID 40040172](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/40040172)
- A 2025 arXiv preprint frames FPCA of vector-valued curves for 3D morphometrics. It is general, not coronary — [arXiv 2509.00650](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.00650)

### Inferences
- The FPCA paragraph's claim that whole-profile methods are untested on coronary curvature (beyond Tello Ayala's neural network) survives. It is safest worded as "curvature profile", given Griffo 2025.
- The citation patterns confirm the earlier conclusion that none of the 2023–2026 geometry papers is "widely cited": the most cited coronary-geometry primary studies (Nannini 2024, OA 44; Zhang 2024, 13; Garcha 2025, 12; Gharleghi 2025, 11) are all in the tens.

### Gaps
- arXiv and medRxiv were not searched systematically for 2025–2026 coronary FPCA preprints. Any found would have to be flagged as preprints anyway.
- Google Scholar counts were not obtained.
