## Per-citation verdicts for every cited key

### Takeaway
Most sentences match their sources. One synthesis sentence is wrong because of how it uses Zebić Mihić 2024: that study's continuous tortuosity index was *higher in non-obstructive than obstructive CAD*, the same inverse direction as the bend counts, so "counts inverse, continuous positive" does not hold. Four other claims are overstated: Zebić Mihić ("only the continuous index detected"; the angle method found the LCX association too), Tommasino ("effect sizes cannot be reproduced"; the point estimate does reproduce, the intervals and p-values do not, and most events were imaging-detected progression rather than revascularization), Nannini (the reported tortuosity is a discrete bend count, and its analyses used manual segmentations), and Griffo ("patients who all went on to have an infarction" is true of the 187-vessel test set only). The rest are correct or have small wording issues.
