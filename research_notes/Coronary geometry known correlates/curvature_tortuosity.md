# Known clinical and anatomical correlates of coronary curvature / tortuosity

Scope: human in-vivo CCTA and invasive angiography. The goal is "known-groups" checks for the CGPS curvature
pipeline (chord-based turning angle per mm at 2.5/5/10 mm, Bullitt distance metric, inflection count,
sum of angles). Several PMC pages were blocked by a bot check, so the evidence for some studies is the
abstract (via Europe PMC) rather than the full text. This is marked where it applies.

## 1. Which patient variables are consistently associated with tortuosity/curvature?

### Takeaway
**Female sex** is the most replicated correlate. It holds in every study found, across both modalities and
all definitions, including one continuous CCTA curvature study in 1/mm. **Hypertension** is the second most
consistent (positive in most studies, null in two). **Age** is weakly positive or null after adjustment.
**BMI** is inverse in the one study that tested it. **Diabetes** is inverse or null. Lipids, smoking and
family history are null.

### Cited Findings
**Sex (female higher):**
- Li et al. 2011, PLoS ONE (n=1,010 Chinese patients with chest pain, invasive angiography; tortuosity = ≥3 bends of ≥45° on a main trunk, present in both systole and diastole). Prevalence 39.1%. Female OR 2.603 (95% CI 1.897–3.607), multivariable logistic. — [Li 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232)
- Chen et al. 2022, BMC Surgery (n=1,280, CCTA, ≥3 consecutive curvatures >45° in systole or diastole). Prevalence 35%. Female OR 1.68 (1.30–2.17), multivariable. — [BMC Surg 2022, PMID 35197040](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12893-022-01470-w)
- JACC: Advances 2026 (n=22,334 patients / 38,691 RCA angiograms; ML segmentation; continuous RCA tortuosity, range 0.007–0.289). Female β = 0.17 SD (p<0.001). — [ML RCA tortuosity, PMID 42312790](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/)
- Sharfo et al. 2024, Clin Physiol Funct Imaging (n=194 from the Danish CATCH trial, CCTA, LAD arc/chord). Female adjusted p=0.01; no effect size given. — [Sharfo 2024, PMID 39210716](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39210716/)
- Groves et al. 2009, W V Med J (n=1,221 angiograms, severe tortuosity = two consecutive 180° turns, visual). More women in the tortuosity group (p=0.039). — [Groves 2009, PMID 19585899](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19585899/)
- Gharleghi et al. 2024, arXiv (n=127 CCTA, no stenosis and zero calcium, Auckland; tortuosity = mean centreline curvature within 10 mm of the LM bifurcation). Female − male curvature: LM +0.062 mm⁻¹ (0.025–0.099), LAD +0.063 mm⁻¹ (0.031–0.096), LCx +0.072 mm⁻¹ (p<0.001 for all), Bonferroni-corrected. Women also had LM/LAD/LCx diameters about 0.6–0.7 mm smaller, a gap that persisted after BMI/BSA adjustment. — [Gharleghi 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12504)
- SCAD case-control (Eleid et al. 2014, Circ Cardiovasc Interv): n=246 SCAD (96% women) vs 313 controls. Tortuosity 78% vs 17%, tortuosity score 4.41±1.73 vs 2.33±1.49. This is a disease-enriched sample and not a general-population check. — [Eleid 2014, PMID 25138034](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCINTERVENTIONS.114.001676)

**Hypertension / blood pressure (positive in most studies):**
- Li 2011: OR 1.533 (1.131–2.076), p=0.006. Hypertension prevalence was similar in both sexes, so it does not explain the sex effect. — [Li 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232)
- Chen 2022 (CCTA): OR 1.35 (1.04–1.75). In women alone, OR 1.54 (1.07–2.22). — [BMC Surg 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12893-022-01470-w)
- JACC Adv 2026: β = 0.06 SD, p=0.002. Large n but a small effect. — [ML RCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/)
- Sharfo 2024 (CCTA, LAD arc/chord): adjusted p<0.001. — [Sharfo 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39210716/)
- El Tahlawi 2016, Atherosclerosis (n=83 CCTA, stable angina, normal/near-normal coronaries): more hypertension in the tortuosity group. — [El Tahlawi 2016, PMID 26828751](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atherosclerosis.2016.01.029)
- Null results: Groves 2009 (hypertension not a predictor) — [Groves 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19585899/); Gharleghi 2024 (no curvature difference by hypertension after Bonferroni, n=127) — [Gharleghi 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12504)
- No study found reports continuous systolic or diastolic BP against tortuosity. Every study used a binary diagnosis.

**Age (weak / inconsistent):**
- Chen 2022: OR 1.02 per year (1.01–1.03), multivariable. — [BMC Surg 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12893-022-01470-w)
- Sharfo 2024: adjusted p=0.045. — [Sharfo 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39210716/)
- JACC Adv 2026: age **not** independently associated after risk-factor adjustment. With the classical arc/chord index, only age and RCA stenosis remained significant. — [ML RCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/)
- Groves 2009: age ≥65 not a predictor. El Tahlawi 2016: no age difference. — [Groves 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19585899/); [El Tahlawi 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atherosclerosis.2016.01.029)
- A search summary credits Tweet/Pellikka in the SCAD population with "about 0.89 units of tortuosity score per decade". This comes from a secondary summary and was not verified against the primary paper. — [SCAD search result](https://mayoclinic.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/coronary-artery-tortuosity-and-spontaneous-coronary-artery-dissec)

**Body size:**
- Chen 2022: BMI inversely associated. BMI 24–27.9 OR 0.76 (0.58–1.00); BMI >28 OR 0.46 (0.31–0.68) vs <24; multivariable. In women, BMI >28 OR 0.51. — [BMC Surg 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12893-022-01470-w)
- No study found tests height or BSA directly against tortuosity. Gharleghi adjusted the diameter sex gap for BMI/BSA but did not report body-size effects on curvature. — [Gharleghi 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12504)

**Diabetes (inverse or null):**
- JACC Adv 2026: β = −0.11 SD, p<0.001. — [ML RCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/)
- Li 2011: diabetes linked to lower tortuosity; the OR is in Table 2 and could not be extracted. — [Li 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232)
- Groves 2009 and El Tahlawi 2016: null. — [Groves 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19585899/)

**Lipids, smoking, family history (null in most studies):**
- JACC Adv 2026: hypercholesterolaemia and smoking not associated. — [ML RCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/)
- Groves 2009: hyperlipidaemia, smoking and family history of CAD not predictors. — [Groves 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19585899/)
- Gharleghi 2024: no curvature difference by smoking. — [Gharleghi 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12504)
- Li 2011: hyperlipidaemia "linked to reduced" tortuosity, and the paper reports smoking inconsistently. — [Li 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232)
- Chen 2022, women only: HDL OR 1.86 (95% CI 1.07–3.24). This is a single subgroup finding. — [BMC Surg 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12893-022-01470-w)

**LV hypertrophy / diastolic dysfunction:**
- The Kahe et al. 2020 review (Coron Artery Dis 31:187–192) lists ageing, hypertension and atherosclerosis as associations and proposes elastin degradation as the mechanism. It calls tortuosity "a benign entity". — [Kahe 2020 summary](https://scholarlyworks.lvhn.org/fellows-residents/101)
- A 2021 Egyptian Heart Journal study ("Is coronary artery tortuosity a precursor of atherosclerosis and/or LV diastolic dysfunction?") reports LVDD and LVH as predictors of tortuosity. The effect sizes were not retrieved. — [EHJ 2021, PMC8319258](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8319258/)
- Dagianti & Fedele 1993: tortuosity linked to concentric hypertrophy, and none of the normal subjects had it. This rests on a secondary search summary only. — (no primary link verified)

**Heart rate, renal function, coronary dominance:** no study found reports an association with tortuosity.

**Physical performance:** in Sharfo 2024, the unadjusted association with METs (p=0.003) vanished after adjustment for age, sex and hypertension (p=0.97). — [Sharfo 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39210716/)

### Inferences
- The sex effect is large in curvature units. Gharleghi's difference of about 0.06–0.07 mm⁻¹ against a coefficient of variation of about 24–26% in LM/LCx curvature suggests roughly 0.5–1 SD (the Table 1 means could not be extracted to confirm). With n≈1,800 the CGPS pipeline should show a clear female > male difference in proximal LM/LAD/LCx curvature. Failing to find one would be a red flag.
- The sex effect may partly reflect vessel calibre and heart size. Women have smaller vessels and hearts, and a smaller radius of the surface the artery wraps around means higher curvature. Checking sex with and without adjustment for BSA/height (or LV size) would separate an anatomical-scale effect from a true sex effect. This is my inference: no study found tests it.
- Hypertension: expect a small positive effect. Adjusted ORs are about 1.35–1.5 for binary visual tortuosity and β ≈ 0.06 SD for continuous measures. In CGPS this is a weaker check, likely needing the full cohort.

### Gaps
- Jakob et al. 1996 could not be located, and its findings are not verified.
- Kahe 2020 full text was not read; only the abstract and secondary summaries were.
- No study found reports continuous BP, height, BSA, heart rate, eGFR, HbA1c or dominance against curvature.

## 2. Is tortuosity inversely associated with atherosclerosis / CAD?

### Takeaway
The evidence is mixed and depends on the definition. Older studies of visual bend counts report an
**inverse** association with obstructive CAD (Groves 2009; Li 2011; Diagnostics 2023). The CCTA calcium
studies are null after adjustment (Chen 2022, Sharfo 2024) or positive (El Tahlawi 2016). The largest
study, continuous ML RCA tortuosity in 22k patients, found a small **positive** association. This is not a
reliable validation check.

### Cited Findings
- Groves 2009: severe tortuosity (12.45% of 1,221) was associated with a lower incidence of significant CAD (p=0.003). — [Groves 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19585899/)
- Li 2011: OR 0.755 (0.574–0.994) for CAD. Tortuosity prevalence was 45.1% without CAD vs 34.4% with CAD. Fewer three-vessel lesions occurred in the tortuosity group. LAD tortuosity was inversely related to LAD atherosclerosis; LCx and RCA showed no relation. — [Li 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232)
- Diagnostics 2023 (n=160, angiography, angle method vs tortuosity index): tortuosity and TI were higher in non-obstructive than obstructive CAD for all three arteries (p<0.01). — [Diagnostics 2023, PMID 38201343](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10795752/)
- Chen 2022 (CCTA): moderate CAC (101–400) OR 1.49 in univariable analysis only. On multivariable analysis there was no association with calcium score or diameter stenosis. — [BMC Surg 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12893-022-01470-w)
- Sharfo 2024 (CCTA): no correlation with calcium score. — [Sharfo 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39210716/)
- El Tahlawi 2016 (CCTA, n=83, normal/near-normal coronaries): tortuosity associated with a higher CAC score. — [El Tahlawi 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atherosclerosis.2016.01.029)
- JACC Adv 2026: CAD OR 1.05 per SD; severe CAD OR 1.09 per SD; Gensini β = 0.05 per SD; all adjusted. With the arc/chord index, by contrast, RCA stenosis was weakly **inverse** (β = −0.03, 95% CI −0.06 to 0.00, p=0.042). — [ML RCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/)
- Nannini et al. 2024 (APL Bioeng, CCTA deep-learning pipeline, bend-count score): higher tortuosity scores were linked to lower calcium volume. — [Nannini 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10807932)
- Haemodynamics (n=127 plaque-free CCTA): higher LAD/LCx curvature was associated with *less* high-OSI and high-RRT area (e.g. LCx β = −0.519 for OSI). The authors call this "potentially atheroprotective". — [Gharleghi 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12504)

### Inferences
- The direction of the effect seems to depend on the metric. Arc/chord and visual bend counts lean inverse, while continuous curvature leans weakly positive. Both are confounded by sex: women are more tortuous and have less CAD. For CGPS, a plaque vs no-plaque comparison is an exploratory result, not a validity check. If it is reported, adjust for sex and age.

### Gaps
- No study found tests tortuosity on a plaque-free baseline against *incident* plaque. CGPS would be the first longitudinal test.

## 3. Within-vessel patterns (proximal / mid / distal; LAD vs LCx vs RCA)

### Takeaway
The vessel ordering is established only for visual bend-count tortuosity on angiography: LCx ≥ LAD ≫ RCA.
**Quantitative per-segment curvature (1/mm) from CCTA by proximal/mid/distal segment is NOT established**
in any source found.

### Cited Findings
- Li 2011 (angiography): tortuosity by vessel was LCx 26.9%, LAD 21.1%, RCA 1.4%; LAD and LCx both tortuous in 9.9%. — [Li 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232)
- Gharleghi 2024: curvature reported for the LM, LAD and LCx only within 10 mm of the LM bifurcation, with a coefficient of variation of LM 25.7% and LCx 23.7%. The table means could not be extracted from the PDF. — [Gharleghi 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12504)
- The Medrano-Gracia computational atlas (CCTA, zero calcium, no stenosis; EuroIntervention 2016) reports diameters, lengths, bifurcation angles and shape PCA. No per-segment curvature values were found in the accessible text. — [Medrano-Gracia atlas](https://eurointervention.pcronline.com/article/a-computational-atlas-of-normal-coronary-artery-anatomy)
- Biplane angiography studies of motion: the RCA and LCx lie in the AV groove, which changes shape little over the cardiac cycle. On the RCA, the site of highest curvature coincides with the site of highest curvature change; on the LAD it does not (Zhu 2003, secondary overview). — [Biplane overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/engineering/biplane-angiography)

### Inferences
- The anatomy suggests (unverified) that the distal LAD wrapping the apex and the LCx/RCA turning around the crux should give higher local curvature distally, and that the proximal RCA has a large-radius curve in the AV groove. This is reasoning, not literature. Do not use it as a validation check without a source.
- Visual "≥3 bends of ≥45°" mostly picks up mid–distal LAD/LCx snaking. This is closest to an inflection or sum-of-angles metric, not to mean curvature.

### Gaps
- No CCTA study found reports curvature by SCCT/AHA segment.

## 4. Cardiac phase (systole vs diastole)

### Takeaway
Curvature changes substantially within the cardiac cycle, mainly on the LAD. The only quantitative data
come from small biplane-angiography studies (1–few patients). The biggest changes are in systole. No CCTA
multi-phase curvature study was found.

### Cited Findings
- Gross, VanFossen & Friedman 1996 (biplane cineangiography, LAD, about 30 time points per cycle): the cyclic change in curvature was 0.25–1.8 cm⁻¹ (= 0.025–0.18 mm⁻¹), depending on the site. — [Duke: curvature changes of the LAD during the cardiac cycle](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/684552); [J Biomech, dynamics of curvature](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0021929098000128)
- Prosi et al. 2004 (CFD with experimentally derived time-varying LAD curvature): curvature changes are largest in systole and small in diastole. Source is a secondary search summary; the primary paper was not retrieved and has no verified link.
- Heart rate changes the timing of 3D coronary motion through non-proportional shortening of systole and diastole (Radiology 2007). Curvature itself was not reported. — [Radiology 2007](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/radiol.2451061791)
- The clinical definitions in Li 2011 and El Tahlawi 2016 require bends to be present in *both* systole and diastole, to exclude phase-induced bends. — [Li 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232); [El Tahlawi 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atherosclerosis.2016.01.029)

### Inferences
- The within-cycle LAD change (up to 0.18 mm⁻¹) is of the same order as, or larger than, the sex difference (about 0.06 mm⁻¹). If CGPS reconstruction phases vary (e.g. systolic reconstructions at high heart rate), phase is a real noise or bias source. Heart rate (preparation) and image quality are the available proxies.

### Gaps
- No multi-patient CCTA study of curvature by phase was found.

## 5. Definitions and agreement between them

### Takeaway
Definitions fall into three families: visual bend counts (≥3 bends of ≥45°; severe = ≥2 consecutive
turns of 180°), arc/chord tortuosity index, and continuous curvature in 1/mm. Curvature-based metrics agree
with each other and outperform arc/chord in tracking haemodynamics. Arc/chord and visual counts sometimes
give opposite CAD associations to continuous curvature.

### Cited Findings
- Kashyap et al. 2022, Sci Rep (127 CCTA without CAD; LM, LAD, LCx): mean absolute curvature had the highest R² with low TAWSS, followed by mean squared derivative of curvature and RMS curvature. The tortuosity index (arc/chord), the most widely used metric, correlated worst. — [Kashyap 2022, PMID 35039557](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-04796-w)
- Bullitt et al. 2003, IEEE TMI (MRA, intracerebral): three 3D metrics, namely the distance metric (arc/chord), inflection count metric (DM × (inflections+1)) and sum of angles metric (SOAM). The inflection count metric was best overall; SOAM was best for tightly coiled "corkscrew" vessels. — [Bullitt 2003, PMID 12956271](https://doi.org/10.1109/tmi.2003.816964)
- Diagnostics 2023: the tortuosity index separated non-obstructive from obstructive CAD and localised ischaemia (LCx TI with lateral, LAD TI with anterior, p<0.001). The angle method found only LCx and lateral ischaemia (OR 4.9, p=0.046). — [Diagnostics 2023](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10795752/)
- JACC Adv 2026: continuous curvature gave a positive CAD association, while arc/chord gave a weak inverse association with RCA stenosis in the same data. — [ML RCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/)

### Inferences
- The CGPS chord-based mean |curvature| at 2.5/5/10 mm is closest to Kashyap's and Gharleghi's "average absolute curvature". Its correlation with Bullitt arc/chord should be moderate, not near 1, so a weak correlation is not by itself a bug. Expect SOAM and the inflection count to track the visual "≥3 bends" definitions more closely.

### Gaps
- No study found compares chord length or scale (2.5 vs 10 mm) for coronary curvature.

## 6. Robust validation checks vs weak checks, mapped to CGPS variables

### Takeaway
Use **female > male** (strong; CGPS `male`) as the primary known-groups check and **hypertension**
(moderate; antihypertensive medication or BP in BL, derived hypertension in FU only) as a secondary one.
Age, BMI and diabetes are only supporting checks. Lipids, smoking, calcium, dominance and heart rate are
not validation checks.

### Cited Findings and mapping
| Correlate | Direction | Strength of evidence | CGPS availability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Female sex | + | Strong, replicated (≥6 studies incl. 22k angiography and 1,280 CCTA; continuous curvature +0.06–0.07 mm⁻¹) — [Gharleghi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12504), [Chen](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12893-022-01470-w), [ML RCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/) | `male` (BL/FU) |
| Hypertension | + | Moderate (OR 1.35–1.53; β 0.06 SD; 2 nulls) — [Li](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232), [Sharfo](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39210716/) | Clinic SBP/DBP, antihypertensive med (BL); derived hypertension **FU only** |
| Age | weak + / null | Inconsistent (OR 1.02/yr; null after adjustment in 22k) — [Chen](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12893-022-01470-w), [ML RCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/) | `scan_age` |
| BMI | − | Single study (OR 0.46 for BMI >28) — [Chen](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12893-022-01470-w) | BMI, height, weight, BSA |
| Diabetes | − / null | Weak (β −0.11 SD in 22k; null elsewhere) — [ML RCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/) | `diabetes`, HbA1c, diabetes medication |
| Lipids, smoking, family history | null | Consistently null — [Groves](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19585899/) | Available; use as negative controls |
| CAC / plaque / CAD | mixed | Inconsistent — see section 2 | Per-vessel CACS, segment reads |
| LVH / diastolic dysfunction | + | Weak, small studies — [EHJ 2021](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8319258/) | **Not available** (no echo); LV mass could be derived from the CT volumes |
| Heart rate, eGFR, dominance | — | No literature found | Available (HR preparation, eGFR, dominance) |
| SCAD / FMD | + | Strong but disease-specific — [Eleid 2014](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCINTERVENTIONS.114.001676) | **Not available** |
| Cardiac phase | changes LAD curvature by up to 0.18 mm⁻¹ | Small biplane studies — [Gross 1996](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/684552) | Phase not in the headers; HR and image-quality grades are proxies |

### Inferences
- Lipids, smoking and family history are consistently null, which makes them useful **negative controls**. A metric that strongly "groups" by LDL or smoking is suspicious.
- Diabetes and BMI are both inverse, and they are correlated. Hypertension is positive but correlated with BMI. A multivariable model (sex, age, hypertension, BMI) mirrors how Chen 2022 and JACC Adv 2026 report their effects, which makes comparison fair.
- Use the 600 plaque CCTAs with care for known-groups checks. Most prior work is in symptomatic patients, and the plaque/tortuosity relation is unsettled.

### Gaps
- The Gharleghi Table 1 means and SDs for curvature were not extracted, so no effect in SD units is available for CCTA curvature.
- No general-population CCTA study of continuous coronary curvature was found. CGPS would be the first.
