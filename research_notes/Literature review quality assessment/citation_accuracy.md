## Do the sentence-level claims match the papers?

### Takeaway
Most claims match. One is a real misattribution: the Bransby main-branch accuracy is an inter-observer result, not a result for the automated methods. Two overstate their sources: van der Giessen is cited for WSS but only assumed it, and "underlying cause" plus "clearly related" go beyond the evidence. Sakellarios "existing plaque", the plural "current risk scores" for SCORE2, and STEMI in Wang are minor wording issues.

### Cited Findings
- Bransby Table 1 is inter-observer agreement per segment: "main branches (LM, LAD, LCx and RCA) were the best segmented (DSC 84.8–95.3)". Automated methods "do not yet match human-level agreement" (CAS-Net differs significantly from inter-observer agreement on DSC, p < 0.001). — local arXiv PDF 2608.30404
- van der Giessen: "High WSS is assumed in part IV ... in part I ... low WSS is expected". There was no CFD. — [EuroIntervention](https://eurointervention.pcronline.com/article/plaque-and-shear-stress-distribution-in-human-coronary-bifurcations-a-multislice-computed-tomography-study)
- Kashyap full-bifurcation R² values were 0.112, 0.101 and 0.093 for the three curvature measures. — [PMC8764056](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8764056/)
- Sakellarios full text does not state that baseline plaque was present in all segments. It reports "disease progression ... plaque area and the necrotic core component were increased". — [EuroIntervention](https://eurointervention.pcronline.com/article/the-effect-of-coronary-bifurcation-and-haemodynamics-in-prediction-of-atherosclerotic-plaque-development-a-serial-computed-tomographic-coronary-angiographic-study)
- Wang covers 208 STEMI patients, not all acute MI. — [Europe PMC 15249505](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/15249505)

### Inferences
- Suggested fixes: (a) rewrite the Bransby sentence and table cell. The methods came close to but did not reach inter-observer agreement, and agreement was highest in the main branches for the annotators. Or drop the main-branch clause and keep only the diameter/attenuation dependence, which also comes from the analyst data. (b) The Bransby-to-Wang argument ("automated segmentation is therefore most reliable in the arteries that matter most") then needs a different basis or should be softened. (c) Cite van der Giessen for plaque location only and Chatzizisis for the WSS mechanism, and change "identified ... as the underlying cause" to "implicated". (d) Change "clearly related" to "significantly related". (e) Change "existing plaque growth" to "plaque progression in patients with established disease".
- Gap 1, line 82–83 ("those that related geometry to flow in patients included at most several hundred individuals"): Kashyap had 127 and Sakellarios 15. Griffo's 748 patients (angiography-based WSS) would also fit "several hundred". The claim is consistent with the cited studies but not exhaustive.

### Gaps
- Garcha's "formed in the narrower branch" was not re-verified against the full text this pass. It relies on the bib note saying the full text was checked.

## Is SCORE2 cited and characterised correctly?

### Takeaway
