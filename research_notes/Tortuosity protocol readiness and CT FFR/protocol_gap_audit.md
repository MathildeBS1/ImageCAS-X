# Tortuosity protocol gap audit: is tortuosity_methods.tex ready for a serial-CCTA plaque-development paper?

Scope and method. I read `tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex` (the PDF source, dated 26 Sep 2026, "provisional, not pre-registered") and `tortuosity/research/CLAUDE.md` in full, all eight reports under `reports/`, the longitudinal-design note, `thesis/project_plan.tex` and `from_superviser.tex`. External checks were limited to reporting standards (GRRAS, TRIPOD+AI, SCCT, QIBA), serial-CCTA methods and the curvature-plaque review literature. No data were read; the Disease column was not opened. Repo links below use `file://` paths. Tags: [REPO] repo document, [FT] full text read, [ABS] abstract or landing page, [SNIP] search summary only, [DERIV] my own derivation from the PDF's formulas, not computed.

Abbreviations: PDF = `tortuosity_methods.tex`; VS = "Vessel specific tortuosity math and tests" report; PP = "Plaque prone regional geometry descriptors" report; NR = "Centerline tortuosity normal ranges" report; IP = "Investigation priorities across reports" report; CH = "Choosing a coronary tortuosity descriptor" report.

## Q1. What in the protocol is already solid?

### Takeaway
The measurement core is sound and well argued: a single, fully specified, length-insensitive estimator (κ_a at a 5 mm chord) computed once per vessel and attributed to regions so they add up, a clear reason to demote whole-vessel arc/chord, a principled 2D/3D theory section, explicit NaN rows, and honest status language. What is solid is the estimator; what is not yet solid is everything that turns it into a hypothesis test (Q2 to Q5).

### Cited Findings
- The PDF gives a complete, reproducible definition: path = longest geodesic through same-label edges, resampling at h = 0.25 mm, exact chord resampling at ℓ = 5 mm, κ_a = Σθ_k / L, with regional attribution Σθ_k/|R| so that regional totals sum to the whole-vessel value, and explicit NaN rows "with the reason" for regions under 10 mm or without their landmark [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)).
- κ_a is nearly length-free (Spearman with length 0.20/0.26/0.32 on LAD/LCx/RCA over 560 training scans) while whole-vessel arc/chord tracks length (0.80/0.84/0.69) and dominance (RCA DM 1.30 left- vs 1.79 right-dominant, p = 4e-13) [REPO] ([NR report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)). The PDF correctly moves DM to "literature only, reported with length and dominance" [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)).
- The theoretical basis for demoting arc/chord is exact: L/D = (1 − V)^(−1/2), so TI ≈ V/2 is a zeroth moment of the tangent spectrum dominated by long wavelengths, and it is extensive in length under a stationary random-curve model (mean 0.095 at 20 mm to 1.33 at 130 mm while κ_a stayed flat) [REPO] ([VS report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)).
- κ_a survives a 20 mm distal truncation (ICC 0.95/0.88/0.93) where TI does not (0.60/0.81/0.83), and truncation is the realistic failure for CAS-Net-derived trees [REPO] ([VS report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)).
- The regional evidence table (regional κ_a nearly independent between regions, whole-LAD κ_a driven by the distal LAD at ρ 0.89) is a genuinely useful justification for regional rather than whole-vessel analysis [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)).
- The outcome literature favours a local mean-curvature measure over arc/chord: in 127 CTCA patients without obstruction, average absolute curvature explained low WSS with adjusted R² 0.196 (LM) and 0.232 (LAD) against about 0 for the tortuosity index (Kashyap 2022, as recorded) [REPO] ([NR report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md); [Kashyap 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-04796-w)).
- The status paragraph already states the key limits honestly: development cohort, no age or sex, no extractor agreement yet, regions chosen on the same scans, freeze and validate before any claim [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)).
- Dropping torsion-based descriptors (SOAM, writhe) is well founded: SOAM reads 7.1 to 7.3 rad/cm on every trunk regardless of shape, and torsion SD scales as σ_n/(κℓ³) [REPO] ([NR report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md); [VS report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)).

### Inferences
- The PDF is a good "Methods: descriptor definition" section. It is not yet a protocol: it defines what is measured but not the hypothesis, the unit of analysis, the covariates, the reliability gates, or how measurement error enters the outcome model.
- The "one estimator for every vessel, vessel-specific interpretation" principle from VS is implicitly followed and should be stated explicitly as a design rule, because reviewers will ask why the RCA is treated differently (5 mm trim) if the estimator is common.

### Gaps
- None of the "solid" items has yet been rerun from committed code; IP records that most earlier numbers lived in `/tmp` scratchpads [REPO] ([IP report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Investigation%20priorities%20across%20reports.md)). I did not check whether the PDF's own numbers come from the committed `tortuosity/research/` scripts it lists, though the PDF names them and their `/work3` outputs.

## Q2. Which findings from the earlier reports are missing from the PDF and should be added before the computation is frozen (ranked)?

### Takeaway
Ranked by how badly the omission would damage a serial-CCTA plaque paper: (1) a reliability evaluation at the PDF's own operating point, including the extractor-agreement test and pre-set gates; (2) a single pre-registered primary hypothesis with direction, unit and multiplicity; (3) measurement-error correction and power; (4) heart-size and cardiac-phase handling; (5) the covariate set and analysis model; (6) bifurcation descriptors at the LM and LAD-D1; (7) missing-landmark, ramus and length-floor rules; (8) the blinding record. Items 1 to 3 are blocking; 4 to 8 are what reviewers would catch in the first round.

### Cited Findings

**Rank 1. Reliability gates, correlated-noise diagnostic and extractor agreement.**
- VS: κ_a at σ = 1 mm "fails a 0.90 gate under 0.5 mm error correlated over 1 mm (point ICC 0.87/0.76/0.71)"; it reaches a lower 95 % bound of 0.90 only at σ = 3 mm on LAD and RCA, never on the LCx [REPO] ([VS report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)).
- VS gives a correlated-noise diagnostic that has never been run: under white jitter the variance of the perpendicular second difference is flat across lags 1 to 4; a rise before the m⁴ signal regime reveals correlated error [REPO] ([VS report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)).
- NR pre-declares gates: κ_a stays primary if per-vessel ICC lower bound ≥ 0.90 at measured noise and at 0.25 mm correlated jitter, point ICC ≥ 0.75 at 0.5 mm correlated jitter, ≥ 0.85 after a 20 mm cut, |ρ| with length ≤ 0.30; otherwise move to ℓ = 8 mm once and stop tuning. A harder method is adopted only if it beats κ_a by ≥ 0.05 ICC on two of three vessels and keeps ≥ 20 % reliable variance unexplained by κ_a, log TI, log L and dominance [REPO] ([NR report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)). The PDF has no gates, only "only if reliable".
- VS adds a bias cap so that "maximise ICC" does not degenerate to "maximum smoothing": |bias| ≤ 10 % on λ = 20 mm and bend-radius phantoms, which limits σ to about 1 mm for ℓ = 3 to 5 mm; and requires ICC computed within each named vessel, never pooled [REPO] ([VS report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)).
- All five reports name extractor agreement (reference vs CAS-Net-derived centerlines on the 160 test scans, ICC and Bland-Altman) as the single untested bottleneck [REPO] ([IP report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Investigation%20priorities%20across%20reports.md)). The PDF lists it under "Open before adoption" but gives no metric, gate or decision rule.
- Regional windows: κ_l5 regional ICC 0.97 to 0.99 under realistic jitter, 0.70 to 0.91 under 0.25 mm iid stress, 0.86 to 0.98 under ±2 mm window shifts; but "a 5 mm chord inside a 10 to 15 mm window leaves only a few independent chords, so regional κ_l5 is close to a single turning measurement" [REPO] ([PP report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)).

**Rank 2. One pre-registered primary per outcome, with direction and multiplicity.**
- IP: "The single primary descriptor is unresolved" across reports; its recommendation is to pre-register one per outcome (κ_a for whole-vessel extremes under objective 9, the LM daughter angle for new plaque under objective 10) with a stated multiplicity correction [REPO] ([IP report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Investigation%20priorities%20across%20reports.md)).
- PP names the LM daughter-to-daughter angle against new plaque in the LM, proximal LAD and proximal LCx as the best-evidenced primary (ICC 0.81 at 0.43 mm RMS iid stress, little size confounding), with regional κ_l5 and the LM Finet ratio as secondaries under Holm or FDR [REPO] ([PP report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)).
- VS: enter κ_a in a mixed model with vessel as a fixed factor, tortuosity-by-vessel interaction and patient random effect, so that "the global test of the tortuosity terms is then one pre-specified primary test, which handles multiplicity" [REPO] ([VS report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)).
- NR: the CAD association "changes sign across definitions and cohorts (OR 0.755 in Li 2011)", so "a pre-registered plaque hypothesis should therefore be two-sided" [REPO] ([NR report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)).
- `tortuosity/CLAUDE.md` fixes the hypothesis as association with non-obstructive, not obstructive, disease [REPO] ([tortuosity/CLAUDE.md](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/CLAUDE.md)). The PDF states no hypothesis at all; its decision table has κ_a primary in "every plaque-relevant region", which is 6 to 9 region tests.

**Rank 3. Measurement-error correction and power.**
- Regression dilution: observed log OR ≈ λ × true log OR, inflating required n by 1/λ² (2.0 at λ = 0.7, 4.0 at λ = 0.5); correct by regression calibration with bootstrap CI and report both naive and corrected ORs [REPO] ([longitudinal note](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/research_notes/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors/longitudinal_design.md); [Frost and Thompson 2000](https://rss.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1467-985X.00164), [ABS]).
- "λ must come from short-interval repeats, not the ten-year pairs, which confound error with change"; candidates: CAS-Net vs expert on the ImageCAS-X test split, two segmentations of one CGPS scan, two reconstructed phases [REPO] ([PP report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)).
- Power: PP's chain gives about 3,450 regions (345 patients) at OR 1.3/SD, 20 % regional incidence, ρ = 0.1, λ = 0.7; NR's Hsieh calculation gives 1,270 to 1,810 participants at OR 1.3 and 36,600 to 52,300 at OR 1.05 (10 % prevalence). IP asks for a sensitivity table over OR ∈ {1.05, 1.1, 1.3} × λ ∈ {0.5, 0.7, 0.9}, "which also defines when a null result is informative" [REPO] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md); [NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md); [IP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Investigation%20priorities%20across%20reports.md)).
- Largest continuous angiographic study: per-SD OR only 1.05 to 1.09 for CAD (22,334 RCA angiograms; Tello Ayala 2026) [REPO] ([IP report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Investigation%20priorities%20across%20reports.md)).

**Rank 4. Heart size and cardiac phase.**
- Determinants a reference range must condition on: female sex (β 0.17 SD; OR 1.68 to 2.60), hypertension (OR 1.35 to 1.53) and small heart size (LV mass index 77.6 vs 121.0 g/m² in tortuous vs non-tortuous) [REPO] ([NR report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)).
- Size handling: "ratios or a size covariate (inter-ostial distance or LM radius), not division, because ratio indexing can create spurious associations (Kronmal 1993)"; fixed-mm windows anchored at landmarks are safer than length-proportional windows [REPO] ([PP report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)). NR proposes tree convex-hull volume or LAD chord as the heart-size proxy [REPO] ([NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)).
- Dimensionless curvature: δ = κ·r is Dean's curvature ratio [DERIV in PP] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)); but IP found "no traceable computation anywhere" for κ·r, so it "should be treated as unmeasured", and radius weighting had been dropped by two earlier reports following Hart 1999 [REPO] ([IP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Investigation%20priorities%20across%20reports.md)).
- Phase: ImageCAS-X phase is 30 to 40 % or 60 to 70 % per scan and unrecorded; systole raises CCTA curvature by 11.1 % (van Zandwijk 2019) [REPO] ([NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md); [van Zandwijk 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7165136/)). Free-wall vessels (LAD, diagonals, OM, PDA) increased mean curvature by 38 % end-diastole to end-systole, groove vessels (RCA, LCx) did not (Liao 2002, 21 arteries, abstract only) [REPO] ([VS](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)). A CCTA review also reports curvature "significantly higher in end-systole for both artery and segment level" [FT, via fetch summary] ([Review, PMC9497479](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9497479/)).
- The PDF mentions "mixed cardiac phase" once in the status line and nothing else.

**Rank 5. Covariates and the analysis model.**
- Model from the longitudinal note: `newplaque ~ z_desc + region_type + age + sex + htn + smoking + ldl + statin_between + interval + scanner_pair + (1 | patient)`, descriptor standardised within region type, Mundlak patient mean to separate between- from within-patient effects, GEE as sensitivity (or primary for a population-averaged OR), complementary log-log with log(interval) offset because two scans give no event time [REPO] ([longitudinal note](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/research_notes/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors/longitudinal_design.md)).
- Additional confounders listed there: diabetes and BMI (PARADIGM predictors), statin use between scans, heart size and dominance, baseline scanner and reconstruction as batch variables; ComBat only as sensitivity because it assumes scanner is not confounded with biology [REPO] ([longitudinal note](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/research_notes/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors/longitudinal_design.md)).
- `tortuosity/CLAUDE.md` rule: "No segment vs segment comparison within a patient"; the Mundlak decomposition is how that rule is implemented statistically [REPO] ([tortuosity/CLAUDE.md](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/CLAUDE.md)).
- Unit: PARADIGM defines outcome per SCCT segment plaque-free at baseline, new plaque = TPV ≥ 1 mm³ at follow-up (9,583 normal segments, 1,162 patients) [REPO, ABS] ([longitudinal note](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/research_notes/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors/longitudinal_design.md)).

**Rank 6. Bifurcation descriptors at plaque-onset sites.**
- First plaque in plaque-free patients: in PARADIGM, 35 % of 334 baseline plaque-free patients developed new lesions and 60 % of those were in the LAD [REPO, ABS] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)). Plaque sits on the lateral walls of bifurcations and the inner aspect of curves [FT, via fetch summary] ([Review, PMC9497479](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9497479/)).
- Measurable on ImageCAS-X: fitcore3 daughter angle stable between 78.0° and 80.8° across smoothing where a 2 mm secant drifts 98.6° to 63.2°; medians LM 80.8°, LAD-D1 61.4°, LCx-OM1 67.1°, crux 64.4°; LM Finet 0.63 with method ICC 0.85 (fails at the crux, 0.44); daughter ratio ICC 0.63 to 0.86 [REPO] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)).
- Objective 7 explicitly names bifurcation angles and dominance alongside tortuosity [REPO] ([project plan](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/thesis/project_plan.tex)). The PDF excludes the LM entirely and has no bifurcation descriptor.
- Kashyap's strongest κ_a–low-WSS association was in the LM (R² 0.196) and LAD (0.232) [REPO] ([NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)), exactly where the PDF's κ_a cannot reach (LM too short, first 5 mm of the LAD blind; see Q5).

**Rank 7. Missing landmarks, ramus, length floor.**
- A ramus flag is "mandatory", because the atlas angle B is 89 ± 21° with an intermediate artery and 75 ± 23° without [REPO, ABS] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)). ### Inferences
- Items 1 to 3 are blocking because they decide whether a null result is interpretable: without λ and a power table a null is uninformative, and without a single declared primary any positive is exposed to a forking-paths critique (9 regions × 3 descriptors × fixed vs landmark windows).
- Heart size matters more for κ_a than the PDF implies. κ_a has units rad/mm, so for two geometrically similar trees scaled by a factor s, κ_a scales as 1/s [DERIV]. Because small hearts are more tortuous in published data and are more common in women, κ_a without a size covariate partly measures heart size and sex. A size covariate (inter-ostial distance, LM radius or tree hull volume), not a ratio, is the reports' consistent recommendation.
- Phase is a real bias, not only noise, for free-wall vessels: an 11 to 38 % systolic rise is larger than most plausible between-group κ_a differences. For CGPS the reconstruction phase and heart rate at baseline must be extracted from DICOM headers and entered as covariates, with a sensitivity analysis restricted to one phase window.
- If the thesis keeps κ_a as the tortuosity primary, the cleanest compromise with PP is: one pre-registered primary test of κ_a (global tortuosity-by-region test in one mixed model) for objective 10, with the LM angle as a co-primary from the separate bifurcation family under Holm, or else choose one. The PDF must record which.

### Gaps
- No source gives within-patient ICC of new-plaque outcomes across segments; ρ = 0.1 in the power chain is an assumption [REPO] ([longitudinal note](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/research_notes/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors/longitudinal_design.md)).
- Whether CGPS baseline scans record phase, heart rate and reconstruction kernel is not established publicly.
- The κ·r pointwise stability claim in PP has no traceable computation (IP), so its reliability is unknown.

## Q3. What would reviewers demand that none of the reports covered?

### Takeaway
Reviewers of a serial-CCTA geometry paper would expect: adherence to STROBE for the cohort, a GRRAS-compliant reliability sub-study (sample-size justification, independence and blinding of repeated measurements, CIs), QIBA-style agreement metrics (repeatability coefficient, wCV, Bland-Altman bias) rather than ICC alone, regions mapped to SCCT segments so the plaque outcome and the geometry share a unit, reproducibility of the plaque outcome reading itself, and TRIPOD+AI if the MACE model is reported. The reports cover ICC and pre-registration but none of these reporting frameworks, and none addresses outcome-reader variability or SCCT mapping in the protocol text.

### Cited Findings
- GRRAS (Kottner et al., J Clin Epidemiol 2011;64:96-106) has 15 items; methods items include: "Explain how the sample size was chosen. State the determined number of raters, subjects/objects, and replicate observations" (6), "Describe the sampling method" (7), "Describe the measurement/rating process (e.g. time interval between repeated measurements, availability of clinical information, blinding)" (8), "State whether measurements/ratings were conducted independently" (9); results item 13: "Report estimates of reliability and agreement including measures of statistical uncertainty" [FT] ([GRRAS, PubMed 21130355 landing](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21376316/); [GRRAS PDF](https://www.acilci.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/Guidelines-for-reporting-reliability-and-agreement-studies-GRRAS-were-proposed.pdf)). GRRAS also notes that when reliability is part of a larger epidemiological study, it should be reported as a quality control "before the main study or by using data of the main study" [FT] ([GRRAS PDF](https://www.acilci.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/Guidelines-for-reporting-reliability-and-agreement-studies-GRRAS-were-proposed.pdf)).
- QIBA metrology (Raunig et al., Stat Methods Med Res 2015;24:27-67) covers three areas: linearity and bias, repeatability, and reproducibility under changed conditions; the repeatability coefficient is "the clinical cut-point used to discern between changes in a biomarker's measurements due to measurement error versus changes that exceed measurement error" [ABS/SNIP] ([Raunig 2015](https://doi.org/10.1177/0962280214537344); [PubMed 24919831](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24919831/)).
- TRIPOD+AI (Collins et al., BMJ, 16 April 2024) updates TRIPOD for regression or machine-learning prediction models, with abstract items including sample size and outcome events, performance with CIs, and study registration [ABS/SNIP] ([TRIPOD+AI, PMC11025451](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11025451/); [expanded checklist](https://www.tripod-statement.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/TRIPODAI-Supplement.pdf)). The project plan's MACE model with and without topology features (objective 10) is a prediction-model study in TRIPOD's sense [REPO] ([project plan](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/thesis/project_plan.tex)).
- SCCT 2014 interpretation and reporting guidelines (Leipsic et al., J Cardiovasc Comput Tomogr 2014;8:342-58) use an 18-segment coronary model, with ramus intermedius as segment 17 and left posterolateral branch as segment 18 [ABS/SNIP] ([PubMed 25301040](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25301040/)). Serial CCTA studies "overwhelmingly match at the level of the named SCCT/AHA segment", and PARADIGM defines new plaque per SCCT segment [REPO] ([longitudinal note](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/research_notes/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors/longitudinal_design.md)).
- Outcome-reading variability is itself a reviewed quantity in serial CCTA: early 64-detector work reported intra-observer interscan correlations of r = 0.93 to 0.97 for total, calcified and non-calcified plaque volumes, and AI-based quantitative CCTA reports interobserver variability below 3 % [SNIP] ([NCPV review, EHJ Imaging Methods and Practice](https://academic.oup.com/ehjimp/article/4/3/qyag115/8772048); [interscan variability study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1934592509004031)); a CLARIFY sub-study quantified interobserver variability among expert plaque readers [SNIP] ([CLARIFY sub-study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0899707122002054)).
- A ten-year serial CCTA cohort already exists: 274 non-CABG patients, mean age 57, 42 % women, median interval 10.2 (8.7 to 11.2) years, progression measured by AI-guided quantitative CCTA as percent atheroma volume; baseline non-calcified plaque was the only plaque-volume predictor; no geometric analysis or reproducibility data reported [ABS] ([EHJ 2023 suppl., ehad655.152](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/44/Supplement_2/ehad655.152/7392901)). This contradicts the longitudinal note's statement that "no population-based cohort with ~10-year repeat CCTA was found": that statement holds only for population-based cohorts; a clinical 10-year cohort exists [REPO] ([longitudinal note](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/research_notes/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors/longitudinal_design.md)).
- No coronary CTA study reports an ICC, CV or Bland-Altman figure for any tortuosity or curvature metric; confirmed in three independent note sets [REPO] ([CH report](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Choosing%20a%20coronary%20tortuosity%20descriptor.md)). A CCTA geometry review lists as unresolved "which exact parameters ... should be used, and to which exact points of the coronary tree the measurements should be performed" [FT, via fetch summary] ([Review, PMC9497479](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9497479/)).

### Inferences
Items reviewers would plausibly demand, none covered by the reports or the PDF:
1. **STROBE-conformant reporting** of the CGPS serial subset: selection into the repeat-scan subset (survivorship and healthy-volunteer bias over ten years), flow diagram of exclusions (non-diagnostic scans, failed labelling, NaN regions), missing-data handling for covariates. The selection of who returned for a second scan after ten years is a first-order bias that no report discusses.
2. **A GRRAS reliability sub-study** as a separate, prospectively specified block: sample size for the ICC (the 80-scan validation split gives wide ICC intervals), independence of the two centerline sources, blinding of any manual step, the ICC form (two-way random, absolute agreement, single measure, as IBSI uses [REPO] ([VS](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md))), and uncertainty.
3. **Agreement, not only reliability.** ICC depends on between-subject spread, so an ICC measured on ImageCAS-X (referred, about half with plaque) does not transfer to a healthier CGPS population with a different spread. Report per-region Bland-Altman bias and limits, wCV and the repeatability coefficient, which are population-independent, and derive CGPS λ from the error variance and the CGPS between-subject variance.
4. **SCCT mapping.** The PDF's regions (RCA 5 to 40 mm; LAD LM to D1) are not SCCT segments. The SCCT proximal LAD and the PDF's "LM to D1" are close, but the RCA fixed-mm windows are not an SCCT unit (I recall the proximal RCA segment is defined as ostium to half the distance to the acute margin, but I did not verify that text in this session). If CGPS plaque is read per SCCT segment, a published mapping from arc-length regions to SCCT segments is required, or the outcome must be re-read on the geometric regions.
5. **Outcome measurement error.** New plaque ≥ 1 mm³ is near the detection floor; reviewers will ask for reader variability or AI-QCT reproducibility at both visits and for how scanner generation change over ten years affects plaque detection (differential outcome misclassification by baseline scanner, which also affects geometry).
6. **Inter-scan reproducibility of the geometry** (two acquisitions or two phases of the same patient), not only perturbation and extractor agreement. The PP longitudinal note identifies same-acquisition phase-to-phase co-registration as the published noise-floor method [REPO] ([longitudinal note](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/research_notes/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors/longitudinal_design.md)).
7. **Public, timestamped pre-registration** (for example OSF) rather than a repo commit, stating that the Disease column was seen in development.
8. **TRIPOD+AI** only if the MACE model is reported: internal validation with patient-grouped resampling, calibration, and incremental value (ΔC, NRI with caveats) over the clinical baseline model.
9. **Sensitivity analyses** reviewers expect: sex-stratified estimates (sex is the strongest known tortuosity determinant), exclusion of vessels with baseline obstructive stenosis anywhere (plaque distorting the centerline), single-phase subset, and an E-value or negative-control outcome for residual confounding.
10. **Code and parameter availability**, since values are defined only at a stated (σ, ℓ) and published curvatures do not transfer between studies [REPO] ([VS](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)).

### Gaps
- I did not open the STROBE checklist itself or the full SCCT 2014 text this session; the SCCT segment definitions above rest on an abstract-level source plus recollection and must be verified before the region-to-segment mapping is written.
- PARADIGM full texts ("how early", radiomics) returned 403, so their exact interobserver, blinding and clustering methods were not retrieved.

## Q4. Is κ_a at 5 mm defensible as a plaque-relevant descriptor, or does it need to be peak curvature or curvature at specific sites?

### Takeaway
Defensible as the reliability-first regional primary, but not as the whole story. The haemodynamic evidence links plaque to local low shear at inner curvatures and at bifurcation side walls, and the only human curvature-WSS evidence is small, sign-ambiguous and region-specific. A regional mean at 5 mm is a reasonable low-pass proxy that is measurable; a peak (largest bend angle) and a site-specific measure (at the LM and LAD-D1) should be pre-declared secondaries, and the hypothesis must be two-sided.

### Cited Findings
- Atherosclerosis is common "in the medial aspect of curved vessels or lateral walls of bifurcations"; smaller curvature radius correlates with "higher exposure to low ESS and lower exposure to oscillatory ESS"; in one study curvature was 16.7 % higher in segments with stenosis and 13.8 % higher in arteries with stenosis; time-averaged curvature and torsion and their cardiac-cycle excursion correlated positively with maximum wall thickness in the RCA [FT, via fetch summary] ([Review, PMC9497479](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9497479/)).
- Plaque prevalence in one CCTA-CFD study was highest in low-ESS (49.6 %) and high-ESS (34.8 %) areas [SNIP] ([PMC4312082](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4312082/)).
- Low local ESS and large plaque burden independently predicted plaque growth in PREDICTION (506 ACS patients, serial IVUS, 6 to 10 months) [SNIP, as recorded] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md); [Stone 2012](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/circulationaha.112.096438)).
- In 39 ASOCA left trees with CFD, diameter was the only geometry-haemodynamics correlate surviving multiple-comparison correction; curvature correlations of 0.5 to 0.65 did not survive, and the Marginal curvature correlation with low-TAESS area was negative (r = −0.60), consistent with curvature-driven helical flow raising WSS [FT preprint, as recorded] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md); [Shen 2025, arXiv 2502.06161](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06161)).
- κ_a (average absolute curvature) explained low WSS at R² 0.06 to 0.23 in 10 mm pieces including the LM and LAD, where arc/chord explained nothing (Kashyap 2022) [REPO] ([CH](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Choosing%20a%20coronary%20tortuosity%20descriptor.md); [Kashyap 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-04796-w)).
- First plaque in plaque-free vessels goes mostly to the proximal LAD (PARADIGM, 60 %) and to lateral bifurcation walls (> 90 % of LM plaques opposite the flow divider in one MSCT study) [REPO, ABS/SNIP] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)).
- The longitudinal note found no published serial-CCTA study relating baseline curvature or tortuosity to new plaque per segment [REPO] ([longitudinal note](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/research_notes/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors/longitudinal_design.md)); my search for such a study also found none (results returned only plaque-based predictors and a mention that "vessel tortuosity" was among adverse geometric characteristics for ACS prediction) [SNIP] ([KJR review](https://kjronline.org/DOIx.php?id=10.3348%2Fkjr.2023.1311)).

### Inferences
- Mean vs peak. A region's new-plaque outcome is triggered by the worst local site, which argues for a peak statistic; but peaks are the least reliable statistic of a noisy field (they select the largest noise excursion). κ_a is the reliability-optimal choice; the largest bend angle, already the PDF's secondary, is the natural peak proxy. The PDF gives no reliability evidence for the largest bend angle per region, and bend definitions depend on κ_min and smoothing (bend counts swing 0.88 to 0.37 on the LAD with settings [REPO] ([NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md))). It needs the same gates as κ_a before it can be a secondary.
- Scale. Low-ESS zones on inner curves extend over a few vessel diameters (roughly 5 to 15 mm in proximal vessels of 3 to 4 mm diameter) [DERIV, not sourced]. A 5 mm chord resolves bends at that scale, but aliases wavelengths near 10 mm (98 % loss). That is acceptable for a regional mean but should be stated as a limitation.
- Site specificity. The PDF covers neither of the two sites where onset concentrates: the LM bifurcation (no κ_a possible, LM median 7.3 mm) and the first 5 mm of the LAD and LCx (blind by construction, see Q5). The bifurcation angle and Finet ratio at the LM and LAD-D1 are therefore not optional extras; they are the only way the protocol reaches the prime onset sites.
- Dimensionless form. The Dean ratio κ·r is the physically meaningful curvature for secondary flow, but IP found it untested, and radius is the largest size confounder (absolute radii ρ 0.56 to 0.75 with LM radius [REPO] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md))). Safer: κ_a primary with radius and a heart-size proxy as covariates, and κ·r as a pre-declared sensitivity.
- Direction. Shen's negative curvature association and the "tortuosity protects against obstruction" literature mean the direction is genuinely uncertain; two-sided testing is required (NR already recommends this).

### Gaps
- No CFD or WSS validation of κ_a at 5 mm on ImageCAS-X or CGPS trees exists; PP lists it only as optional priority 6.
- Spatial co-location (is new plaque on the inner curve?) cannot be tested with segment-level outcome reads; it would need plaque position along arc length and circumferentially, which CGPS reading may not provide.

## Q5. Is anything in the PDF wrong or inconsistent with the reports?

### Takeaway
Yes, five points need fixing before freezing: (1) the adopted smoothing (σ = 0 extra) has no reliability evidence, because every quoted ICC was computed at σ = 1 mm or on a different estimator; (2) regional κ_a divides by the full region length although the first 5 mm of every path carries no turning angle, which biases short proximal LAD and LCx regions downward in a length-dependent way; (3) the PDF's own recommendation (fixed proximal windows primary) contradicts its region table (landmark windows); (4) the region definitions differ from the regional report's 2 to 17 mm windows without explanation; (5) the "added information" table overstates f_twist, because unreliable variance counts as "not explained".

### Cited Findings
- **Smoothing mismatch.** PDF: "No extra smoothing ... Adding σ = 1 mm changed κ_a medians by 2 to 4 % (Spearman 0.98 to 0.99)" [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)). VS's headline recipe is "Gaussian smoothed at σ = 1 mm", and every VS ICC table is "ICC(2,1) at σ = 1 mm" [REPO] ([VS](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)); PP's regional ICCs are "κ_l5 at σ = 1 mm" [REPO] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md)); IP's frozen list says "Tortuosity is κ_a at σ = 1 mm, ℓ = 5 mm" [REPO] ([IP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Investigation%20priorities%20across%20reports.md)). VS also shows "ICC rises monotonically with σ and ℓ under every jitter family" [REPO] ([VS](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)), so reliability at σ = 0 is expected to be lower than the σ = 1 figures, which already failed the correlated-jitter gate. NR is the only report that chose σ = 0, and it reports correlated-jitter ICC 0.81/0.74/0.74 for κ_a without stating its σ [REPO] ([NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)); the PDF quotes "κ_a held 0.74 to 0.81" from there.
- **Reliability wording.** The PDF says κ_a "held 0.74 to 0.81" under 0.5 mm correlated jitter as if that were a pass; NR's own gate for that perturbation is point ICC ≥ 0.75, which the LCx and RCA (0.74) miss, and VS's gate is 0.90 [REPO] ([NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md); [VS](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Vessel%20specific%20tortuosity%20math%20and%20tests.md)).
- **Regional denominator.** PDF: chord vertices start at v_0 = x_0; θ_k is defined at v_k for k ≥ 1; κ_a(R) = Σ_{k: s(v_k) ∈ R} θ_k / |R|; it "cannot see a bend closer than one chord to the path end"; LAD proximal = LM to D1 with D1 "median 18 mm from the LM" [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)).
- **Internal contradiction on proximal windows.** PDF table: LAD proximal "LM to D1", LCx "LM to OM1"; PDF text: "Recommendation (untested for LAD and LCx): fixed proximal windows as primary, landmark segments as secondary"; decision table: κ_a primary in "every plaque-relevant region" without saying which scheme [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)).
- **Window definitions differ across documents.** PP's proximal windows are 2 to 17 mm from the LM bifurcation (LAD, LCx) and from the ostium (RCA); the PDF uses 5 to 40 mm for the RCA and landmark segments for LAD and LCx [REPO] ([PP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Plaque%20prone%20regional%20geometry%20descriptors.md); [PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)).
- **Added-information table.** PDF: f_twist has 94 to 99 % of rank variance "not explained" by (κ_a, length), with the caveat "some of the apparent new information may be estimation noise"; yet whole-vessel f_twist ICC is 0.15 to 0.32 under 0.5 mm correlated jitter [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)). NR's adoption rule is on "reliable variance unexplained" [REPO] ([NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)).
- **Confound claim.** PDF: κ_a "not affected by dominance" (all p ≥ 0.2) [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)); the training split has only 32 left-dominant and 15 co-dominant scans [REPO] ([NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)).
- **Status of the development split.** PDF: "confirm on the validation split"; NR: all 560 training scans have been read, so confirmation can only be the 80-scan validation transfer test [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex); [NR](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Centerline%20tortuosity%20normal%20ranges.md)). - **Harmonising other sources.** PDF: CAS-Net and CGPS centerlines "must first pass through the same skeleton plus σ = 0.5 mm step" [REPO] ([PDF](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/tortuosity/research/tortuosity_methods.tex)). The repo has no labelled centerline extractor; "the only skeleton code is skimage inside utils/metrics.py, which produces no labelled tree" [REPO] ([IP](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/reports/Investigation%20priorities%20across%20reports.md)).

### Inferences
- **Smoothing (fix 1).** Either rerun the full perturbation battery (iid, 0.25 and 0.5 mm correlated, truncation, window shift, the lag diagnostic) at the PDF's operating point (σ = 0 extra on the delivered σ = 0.5 mm curve), or adopt σ = 1 mm as VS, PP and IP did. The PDF's own evidence (2 to 4 % median change, Spearman 0.98 to 0.99) means switching costs almost nothing in value but may buy reliability. Whatever is chosen, the PDF must not quote ICCs from a different operating point.
- **Denominator (fix 2)** [DERIV]. With θ_k attributed at s(v_k) = 5k mm (approximately, exactly on a straight path), no angle is ever attributed to [0, 5) mm. For the LAD proximal region [0, D1) with D1 = 18 mm, only vertices at about 5, 10 and 15 mm contribute, and dividing by 18 instead of about 13 mm of covered arc biases the value down by about 28 %; for D1 = 12 mm (2 angles) by about 42 %. The bias depends on region length, i.e. on D1 position, which reintroduces exactly the length dependence κ_a was chosen to avoid. It also means every value is quantised: a 10 to 15 mm region holds 1 to 3 angles. The RCA avoids the problem only because its region starts at 5 mm. Remedies: start every proximal window at 5 mm (as the RCA does), divide by the arc actually covered by attributed vertices, or attribute each θ_k over its two adjoining chords. Separately, the vertex grid is anchored at x_0, so its phase relative to D1 or OM1 varies between patients and adds quantisation noise at region edges.
- **LM/first-5-mm blindness.** The PDF's claim that κ_a "ignores an ostial hook by construction" also means it ignores the first 5 mm of the LAD and LCx, which is the proximal part of the prime onset segment. That should be stated as a limitation and covered by the bifurcation descriptors.
- **Windows (fixes 3 and 4).** Choose one scheme for the primary and record why; the fixed-mm option aligns with PP's rule that fixed-mm windows anchored at landmarks are safer than proportional ones, while the landmark option aligns better with SCCT segments (Q3). Given 127 LAD and 126 LCx landmark regions under 10 mm, the fixed window is likely the more reliable primary, but this is exactly the "untested" item and should be decided on reliability, not on outcome.
- **f_twist (fix 5).** Replace "share not explained" with a disattenuated version (unexplained variance times ICC) or simply state that at ICC 0.15 to 0.32 most of it is noise; demote to exploratory until the extractor test.
- **Dominance claim.** Replace "not affected by dominance" with the estimate and CI; with 32 left-dominant scans p ≥ 0.2 does not show absence.
- **Crux.** CLAUDE.md says tortuosity in the crux window is "near zero" and that window is for bifurcation measures only, while the PDF table lists it as the RCA distal region for κ_a; the decision table then marks it "descriptive". Harmless, but the region table should say so.

### Gaps
- I did not rerun anything, so the size of the σ = 0 reliability loss and the actual denominator bias across the 560 scans are unquantified; both are quick checks on training data only.
- I did not verify whether `regional.py` implements the attribution exactly as the PDF formula states (for example, whether it already uses covered arc as denominator); the bias above is derived from the PDF text, and the code should be checked first.
