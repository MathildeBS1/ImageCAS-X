# Freeze κ_a, then let CGPS define normal

> **Verdict**
>
> **Worth one week: yes, but only on a frozen measurement package, not on a new method.** The week should deliver one committed per-vessel recipe (3D and projected 2D arc/chord, κ_a at 5 mm chord, bend counts with declared floor, plus two Bishop-spectrum shape fractions as exploratory secondaries), a dated pre-registration, reliability against pre-set gates, and development-sample reference distributions for LAD, LCx and RCA with length- and dominance-conditional centiles.
>
> **Not worth a week: making the rigorous 2D/3D method the primary.** Its magnitude kB is κ_a in different clothes (Spearman 0.85 to 0.97) and is less robust to jitter. Its value is a theory section plus two near-orthogonal shape axes (f_wiggle, f_twist), about one to two days.
>
> **Needs more research before any "normal" claim:** (1) the CAS-Net-versus-reference centerline error spectrum, which decides every smoothing scale; (2) normative tortuosity by age, sex and heart size, which no CTA study provides and ImageCAS-X cannot (no age, sex or risk-factor columns, 48.5 % plaque, referred patients); (3) the cardiac-phase effect; (4) a main-vessel labeller for unlabelled CGPS trees; (5) any adjusted continuous-tortuosity outcome association. ImageCAS-X numbers are "development distributions", never "normal ranges".

Using centerlines alone, the thesis should measure tortuosity per vessel (LAD, LCx, RCA; LM is too short and is described by length and bifurcation angle instead) with a mean local bending measure, κ_a at 5 mm chord on the delivered (already smoothed) centerline, as the primary, because it is the only candidate that is length-insensitive, truncation-robust and near log-normal on 560 ImageCAS-X training scans. Literature comparability comes from two cheap companions: whole-vessel arc/chord (3D and projected into a standard angiographic view, which reproduces published 2D medians within 0.06) and bend counts, whose prevalence swings from 0.88 to 0.37 on the LAD with smoothing and floor and so must always carry its settings. The rigorous unification of 2D and 3D is the Bishop curvature spectrum: the complex curvature ψ = k₁ + i·k₂ in a parallel-transport frame reduces exactly to signed planar curvature on a planar curve, and its bending energy splits exactly into course, wiggle, planar and out-of-plane parts. On 150 training scans only its two dimensionless fractions carry information beyond κ_a and length. The reason to stop at a week is that the decisive unknowns are data questions: the realistic extractor error decides whether any shape fraction survives, and "normal" is a population question that only CGPS, with age, sex and heart size, can answer.

## The recipe: three vessels, five numbers each, all at declared scales

**Segments.** For each scan, take the longest geodesic path through points carrying label LAD (2), LCx (3) or RCA (9) with `tortuosity/vessels.py`, oriented proximal to distal from the VTK `start_points`. The segment is the whole labelled trunk, ostium to label end, which matches the literature's "ostium to the smallest visible branch" rather than any AHA segment ([Zebić Mihić 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10795752/)). **LM is excluded from tortuosity** (median length 7.3 mm, DM 1.011, κ_a undefined in 72 % of cases) and reported as length plus bifurcation angle. **The delivered centerlines are already smoothed**: the dataset paper regenerated them by skeletonising the corrected masks, then Gaussian smoothing with σ = 0.5 mm over a sliding window of 5 vertices. So resample at 0.25 mm arc length and add **no further smoothing** (σ = 0) for the primary; extra σ = 1 mm and 2 mm are declared sensitivities only. On all 560 training scans, adding σ = 1 mm changes κ_a@5 medians by 2 to 4 % with Spearman 0.98 to 0.99 against σ = 0 (0.94 to 0.97 at σ = 2 mm), and arc/chord by about 1 % with Spearman 0.999; bend counts are the only measure that moves (Spearman 0.64 to 0.78 at σ = 1 mm) (`compute_simple.py 0 _sigma0`, `compute_simple.py 0.5 _sigma05`). **Any centerline not from the dataset** (CAS-Net predictions, CGPS) must pass through the same skeleton plus σ = 0.5 mm, 5-vertex step before measurement, so every source carries identical smoothing. Apply a 20 mm length floor, to be replaced by the ICC-versus-remaining-length curve on day 3.

**Formulas.** With L the smoothed arc length and D the end-to-end chord:

| Output | Definition | Role |
|-|-|-|
| DM | L / D; report TI = DM - 1 and log(DM - 1) | literature comparison (3D) |
| DM2 | L/D of the centerline projected on the plane normal to d = (sin α cos β, -cos α cos β, sin β), α = LAO angle (RAO negative), β = cranial (caudal negative); LAD RAO 20 / CRA 30, LCx RAO 20 / CAU 30, RCA LAO 30; mean over 256 anterior views as sensitivity | literature comparison (2D angiography) |
| κ_a | resample at chord ℓ = 5 mm, u_j unit chord vectors, θ_j = arccos(u_j · u_(j+1)), κ_a = Σ θ_j / L (mm⁻¹); ℓ = 8 mm is the pre-declared fallback | **primary** |
| bends | maximal runs of 1 mm chord vertices turning more than floor × 1 mm, floor 0.02 mm⁻¹ (radius under 50 mm), split where the binormal flips; bend angle = cumulative turning; report n ≥ 45°, n ≥ 90°, n ≥ 180°, and the binary "≥ 3 bends ≥ 45°"; floor 0.10 mm⁻¹ with σ 2 mm as the angiography-like sensitivity | descriptive only |
| f_wiggle, f_twist | Bishop curvature spectrum, below | exploratory secondaries |

Do not report Bullitt's full SOAM or ICM from these centerlines: SOAM with torsion reads 7.1 to 7.3 rad/cm on every trunk regardless of shape, 7 to 10 times its in-plane part, because torsion between consecutive 1 mm osculating planes is noise ([Bullitt 2003](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2430603/); `summary.txt` below).

**The rigorous 2D/3D method (Bishop curvature spectrum, BCS).** Transport a frame (T, M₁, M₂) along the curve by the minimal rotation from T_(i-1) to T_i (Rodrigues), so that T′ = k₁M₁ + k₂M₂ ([Bishop 1975](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00029890.1975.11993807); [Wang et al. 2008](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/computation-rotation-minimizing-frames/)). Define ψ = k₁ + i·k₂, so |ψ| = κ and the phase advances at the torsion rate. Smooth with σ0 = 1.25 mm (2 mm as sensitivity). Then:

- E = ∫ |ψ|² ds, and kB = √(E / L) in mm⁻¹.
- Course/wiggle: take the orthonormal DCT of ψ, weight power by |H_L(ω)|² = exp(-ω²σc²) with σc = √(ln 2) · λc / (2π), λc = 20 mm; E_course + E_wiggle = E exactly by Parseval. f_wiggle = E_wiggle / E.
- Planar/twist: with g_W a Gaussian of sd W = 5 mm, E_planar = ∫ |g_W * ψ²| ds and E_twist = ∫ (g_W * |ψ|² - |g_W * ψ²|) ds ≥ 0. f_twist = E_twist / E. Guard E = 0 (straight input returns NaN otherwise).

This is the principled merge of 2D and 3D because a planar curve gives a real ψ equal to the signed planar curvature (so Grisan's constant-sign turns are its sign-constant intervals, [Grisan 2008](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2007.904657)) and exactly zero twist for any number of inflections, while a helix gives f_twist = 1 - exp(-2τ²W²). Every classical index becomes a moment of the ψ power spectrum: RMS curvature is moment 0, Grisan's density is approximately turn-length-weighted energy (moment near -1), and arc/chord is approximately Σ P(ω)/ω² / 2L (moment -2), which explains in one line why arc/chord is dominated by long wavelengths, length and truncation. Mean |κ| is a first absolute moment and cannot be split, which is the structural trade-off: only the energy form decomposes exactly, and the energy form weights noise more.

**Numbers to report per vessel.** Median, IQR, 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles with 90 % CIs, skew on raw and log scale, n and number excluded with reason, Spearman ρ with L, and stratified medians by dominance and Image Quality. All at the stated (σ, ℓ, floor, view, σ0, λc, W); values at other scales are not comparable (f_twist medians move from 0.22 to 0.45 as W goes from 2.5 to 10 mm, while ranks hold at ρ ≥ 0.90).

**Reference ranges.** Follow CLSI EP28-A3c: nonparametric 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles with 90 % binomial CIs, which needs at least 120 per partition ([analyse-it EP28 guide](https://analyse-it.com/learn/choosing-a-reference-interval-method); [CLSI EP28](https://clsi.org/shop/standards/ep28/)). Dominance cannot be a partition (32 left-dominant and 15 co-dominant training scans), so it enters as a covariate. For DM, publish centiles conditional on log L and dominance by quantile regression (τ 0.025, 0.5, 0.975) or GAMLSS/LMS with a Box-Cox distribution ([Cole 2009](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/sim.3504)); this answers "tortuous for its length". For κ_a a log-scale parametric interval is defensible (log skew -0.03 to 0.52) with the nonparametric interval as check. Flag outliers above the conditional 97.5th centile and a "top 5 %" group above the 95th, following the decile convention of the largest continuous study ([Tello Ayala 2026](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jacadv.2026.102829)). All 560 training scans have now been read once by the simple pass, so after freezing, the only honest check is the EP28 transfer test on the 80 validation scans (about 20 per partition, count how many fall outside); test scans stay untouched. On CGPS the same machinery becomes a true reference: pre-declare "normal" as either the whole population or a plaque-free, risk-factor-free subgroup, and fit LMS curves in age, sex and a heart-size proxy.

**Projecting the 3D centerline into a standard view lands on the published angiographic numbers.** The obstructive-CAD group of the only per-vessel continuous angiographic study has TI medians LAD 1.18 (1.14 to 1.26), LCx 1.15 (1.10 to 1.30), RCA 1.87 (1.65 to 2.17) ([Zebić Mihić 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10795752/)), against our DM2 of 1.20, 1.16 and 1.81. Since that study did not state its views and used diastolic frames, this is an external validity check, not a calibration. 2D and 3D DM rank vessels almost identically (ρ 0.90 to 0.97), so the 2D number adds comparability, not information. κ_a medians of 0.045 to 0.057 mm⁻¹ also sit near the 0.050 to 0.066 mm⁻¹ mean curvature measured in normal LAD and RCA from biplane angiography ([Zhu 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2759354/)), though the scales differ.

**Arc/chord is a length and dominance variable.** RCA DM is 1.30 in left-dominant versus 1.79 in right-dominant scans (p = 4e-13), LCx 1.48 versus 1.27, LAD 1.47 versus 1.33, while κ_a barely moves (RCA p = 0.20, LAD p = 0.59). Image Quality is associated with nothing (|ρ| ≤ 0.07). **Bend counts do not port from angiography.** The fraction with "≥ 3 bends ≥ 45°" is 0.88/0.60/0.53 (LAD/LCx/RCA, 3D) at σ 1 mm and floor 0.02 mm⁻¹, falling to 0.37/0.19/0.07 at σ 2 mm and floor 0.10, whereas angiography reports LCx 26.9 %, LAD 21.1 % and RCA 1.4 % ([Li 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232)). No setting restores the angiographic LCx > LAD order, because a 3D centerline records the LAD's long curve over the anterior wall and apex as large bends that a grader does not count as abrupt turns. Calibrating a floor would need expert grades that do not exist.

## The Bishop spectrum adds two shape axes, not a better magnitude

BCS was implemented in `tortuosity/research/unified/bishop.py`, verified on phantoms with `synthetic.py`, run on the first 150 training ids with `cohort.py` and analysed with `analyze.py`; outputs are `/work3/s254124/imagecasx_results/tortuosity_research/unified/synthetic.txt`, `cohort_train150.csv` (450 vessels, 0 failures) and `analysis_train150.txt`. These 150 ids are the earlier P150 set, so none of this is out-of-sample.

**The mathematics holds.** Rotation, mirror, reversal and resampling leave kB at 0.1433; planar curves give zero twist; helices match the predicted f_twist (0.510 versus 0.524, 0.088 versus 0.088); the four energy parts sum to E to six digits; and kB is monotone in both amplitude and frequency of a sine, where κ_a at 5 mm turns over with amplitude and aliases to 0 at λ = 10 mm. The noise bias is additive and closed-form in the linear regime, b_iid = 3σ_n²h / (4√π σ0⁵), matching Monte Carlo (7.09e-4 versus 6.85e-4 at σ_n 0.06 mm). σ0 = 1.25 mm is bracketed from first principles: at least 1.16 mm keeps iid bias under 5 % of signal at the measured 0.06 mm noise, and at most 1.27 mm keeps 70 % of a 90° bend of 3 mm radius.

The magnitude brings nothing new, but the fractions do: **the RCA's bending energy is three-quarters course**, consistent with its groove-following C shape, while LAD and LCx split about evenly, and about a quarter of every trunk's energy is out of the local bending plane. At the measured 0.06 mm noise every BCS output has ICC ≥ 0.96. Under 0.5 mm correlated error the fractions collapse to 0.15 to 0.60, and no σ0 can satisfy both the bias and the retention constraints there: bias under 20 % of signal needs σ0 of about 3 mm, which keeps only 13 % of a 3 mm-radius bend. Whether CAS-Net-derived centerlines live in the 0.06 mm or the 0.5 mm regime is the single unknown that decides whether the shape axes are usable at all.

## Local bending is also what the outcome evidence favours

The two head-to-head comparisons of definitions both favour a local curvature average over whole-vessel arc/chord. In 127 CTCA patients without obstruction, average absolute curvature explained low wall shear stress with adjusted R² 0.196 (LM), 0.232 (LAD) and 0.112 (whole bifurcation, p = 0.001), against about 0 for the tortuosity index, and it was the closest to normally distributed (skew 1.02 versus 2.71) ([Kashyap 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-04796-w)). In 38,691 angiograms a local turning-angle profile reached AUC 0.67 for CAD against 0.60 for the global score, with a per-SD odds ratio of only 1.05 ([Tello Ayala 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/)). The strongest effects are in rare conditions: tortuosity in 78 % of SCAD patients versus 17 % of controls ([Eleid 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25138034/)); the CAD association changes sign across definitions and cohorts (OR 0.755 in [Li 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0024232)). A pre-registered plaque hypothesis should therefore be two-sided.

The determinants that a normal range must condition on are female sex (β 0.17 SD; OR 1.68 to 2.60), hypertension (OR 1.35 to 1.53) and small heart size (LV mass index 77.6 versus 121.0 g/m² in tortuous versus non-tortuous hearts) ([Tello Ayala 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308244/); [PMC8867736](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8867736/); [Oehler 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5912164/)). ImageCAS-X supplies none of them: its 1000 patients come from one hospital with prior stroke, TIA or PAD, and `Descriptors.xlsx` holds only Image Quality, Dominance and Disease ([ImageCAS-X paper](file:///zhome/e2/6/224426/project/ImageCAS-X/ImageCAS-X_%20a%20dataset%20and%20benchmark%20for%20coronary%20artery%20segmentation%20and%20centerline%20extraction%20in%20coronary%20CT%20angiography.pdf)). Its phase is 30 to 40 % or 60 to 70 % per scan and unrecorded, and systole raises CCTA curvature by 11.1 % ([van Zandwijk 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7165136/)). CGPS is not disease-free either: 46 % of its 9533 asymptomatic CCTA participants have some CAD ([Fuchs 2023](https://www.acpjournals.org/doi/10.7326/M22-3027)).

Power sets the ceiling on what the outcome step can find. For logistic regression at α 0.05 and power 0.80 with 10 % prevalence ([Hsieh 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9699234/)), a per-SD OR of 1.30 needs about 1,270 participants at perfect reliability and 1,810 at reliability 0.7, while the realistic OR of 1.05 needs 36,600 to 52,300. A CGPS-sized cohort is therefore underpowered for small effects on rare outcomes, and every unit of reliability lost to the extractor costs sample size directly.

## Conclusion

The one-week package is: day 1, commit `topology/{io,paths,coords}` and the untracked `tortuosity/research/` scripts with their outputs, retire the Frenet curvature in `topology/tortuosity.py`, and write the pre-registration (recipe above, gates below, Disease still unopened); day 2, rerun the frozen recipe and fit the conditional centiles; day 3, the perturbation battery with per-vessel ICC(2,1) and bootstrap CIs, plus the length-floor curve; day 4, the added-information test for f_wiggle and f_twist and a heart-size proxy (tree convex-hull volume or LAD chord); day 5, write the measurement section. Pre-declared gates: κ_a stays primary if its per-vessel ICC lower bound is at least 0.90 at measured noise and 0.25 mm correlated jitter, point ICC at least 0.75 at 0.5 mm correlated jitter, at least 0.85 after a 20 mm cut, and |ρ| with length at most 0.30; if it fails, move to ℓ 8 mm once and stop tuning. A harder method is adopted only if it beats κ_a by at least 0.05 ICC on two of three vessels and keeps at least 20 % reliable variance unexplained by κ_a, log TI, log L and dominance (these thresholds are judgement calls to be justified in the pre-registration, following [Koo and Li 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4913118/)).

What changed is where the difficulty sits. The measurement is essentially settled, and the mathematics now explains why arc/chord is confounded and κ_a is not. What is unsettled is not computable on ImageCAS-X: normal tortuosity is a function of sex, blood pressure and heart size that no CTA study has charted, and the shape axes live or die on an extractor error that has not been measured. The thesis gains most by stopping method work after this week, measuring CAS-Net-versus-reference agreement on the 160 test scans, and producing the first continuous per-vessel CTA reference curves on CGPS.
