# Fixing the sign, the noise, and the CNN

The thesis feature document (`thesis/risk_models/01_model_features.tex`) has sound algebra but three real problems: a pre-specified sign for Delta_HK that its own sources do not support, radius features whose measurement error has never been quantified, and a Model 2 that the expected event count cannot support. **Little needs dropping for correlation reasons.** The only exact duplicate, the Finet ratio F, is already excluded from the models. The bigger correlation risk is measurement noise shared between bifurcations, not the pairs the document lists. On models, Model 1 should stay as primary, but as a penalised logistic regression with a clinical-only baseline (Model 0). The CNN should be demoted to an exploratory analysis and replaced as the main comparator by a low-dimensional functional summary of the curvature profile. Status labels below matter. Algebra, source-reading and citation checks are **confirmed** (against fetched pages or the repo's `refs.bib` notes). The bifurcation correlation numbers are **simulation only**: the 150-case extraction script `/work3/s254124/risk_feature_review/bif_features.py` was tested on 3 cases (about 4 s each) and never run at scale, so no real bifurcation feature table exists. The curvature correlations, by contrast, come from real tables of 560 training cases.

## The Delta_HK sign is the most exposed claim, and the sources point both ways

The arithmetic holds. The Huo-Kassab optimum Gamma_HK = 2^(-3/7) = 0.743 is correct whatever the asymmetry, the risk-dependence equation reduces to Finet's 0.678 at a symmetric split (F = 0.673), and the centering identity and VIF example are right (**confirmed** by calculation). The trouble is the mechanism wrapped around the feature. The shear argument for a negative Delta_HK leans on Schoenenberger's calcium result, but that study defined its ratio by Murray's law with exponent 3, under which shear is constant across a bifurcation ([Europe PMC 22261173](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:22261173%20AND%20SRC:MED&resultType=core&format=json)). Under the exponent 7/3 that the thesis uses, shear scales as r^(-2/3), so a bifurcation sitting exactly on the HK law is not shear-neutral, and a healthy symmetric split has a Murray-3 ratio above 1 (parent 1.346 r1 against 1.26 r1). The finding is also cross-sectional dense calcium in 253 diseased IVUS patients, not new plaque (**confirmed**).

The second source pulls the other way. Witt found the optimality ratio *rose* under NO blockade, which is a positive deviation, in six healthy male volunteers (p = 0.03), in retinal arterioles ([PMC2954284](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2954284/)). The thesis says departure "in either direction" is linked to disease, then pre-specifies a one-sided negative sign in its tables. If both stories held, a signed linear term would let the two directions cancel, and the feature should be unsigned or quadratic. The endothelial inference ("the ratio therefore tracks endothelial function") rests on n = 6 and does not transfer to epicardial vessels (**confirmed**). A related mismatch: the Delta_HK mechanism is low shear on the wide left main wall, but the left main is excluded from the outcome, and the outcomes cover whole LAD and LCx domains while shear acts locally (**confirmed** from the document's own definitions).

Three further points weaken the caliber family. First, the scale-invariance claim covers only multiplicative error. Segmentation blur, thresholding and EDT discretisation are additive offsets, and in illustrative radii (invented by the audit, not measured) a plus or minus 0.25 mm offset moved Delta_HK for a left main by about 0.03 to 0.06, the same size as the signal (**confirmed** logic, illustrative numbers). Second, my simulation found that a *constant* additive bias did not hurt reliability or reintroduce size dependence in a simple setting, so the concern is real but its size is open until perturbation on real segmentations is done (**simulated**). Third, Shen 2025 found that absolute diameter, not ratios, survived correction for shear, and absolute radius is exactly what the design removes (**confirmed** against the bib note). Garcha's CFD result (230 synthetic geometries) is U-shaped in the LAD/LCx ratio with a neutral point near 0.94 and separate curves by dominance, whereas the thesis enters lambda linearly with no dominance interaction ([Garcha 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11770140/)).

## Cited studies carry more weight than they can bear

Several motivating citations are used for claims stronger than what they showed (all **confirmed** against fetched pages or bib notes unless marked). Stone's PREDICTION study found that low shear predicted the *secondary* endpoint of lumen narrowing, not the primary plaque-growth endpoint, in 506 post-ACS patients, so the opening sentence "plaque starts where the wall feels low shear" is progression evidence, not initiation evidence. ICONIC tested plaque *location* (distance from ostium, bifurcation site, binary tortuosity) in precursor lesions, not angle or caliber descriptors ([ACC journal scan](https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/Journal-Scans/2022/02/04/18/53/Association-of-Plaque-Location)). The document's "five angle studies" are really four, of which three are clinical association studies (Temov reports only an angle distribution, Wang is a 76-artery shape study), and Juan found no association after adjustment. Sakellarios had 17 bifurcations. Taylor's pooled exponent of 2.39 (2.24 to 2.54) has I-squared of 99% and is a flow-diameter exponent for larger vessels; it excludes 3 but is equally compatible with 2.5 ([Taylor 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11380967/)). Tello Ayala is angiographic 2D with a same-reader CAD label, 78.7% segmentation success and no significance test, which is weak support for "motivates Model 2 directly" (the document's own hedge on this study is the right model for the others).

The Ogundimu citation for 20 events per weight is misapplied. The paper is a Cox-regression resampling study that found 20 events necessary for *low-prevalence binary predictors*, with continuous predictors adequate at 10 ([PMC5045274](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5045274/)). The evidence for any fixed EPV rule is weak ([van Smeden 2016](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12874-016-0267-3), [van Smeden 2019](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0962280218784726)). Smaller items worth fixing: F should be compared with an asymmetry-dependent HK value (0.673 at rho 1, 0.720 at 0.5, 0.789 at 0.3) rather than the fixed 0.678, so a deviation at a D1 split is not evidence of bad radii; the curvature terms are not scale-free even though the ratio section praises scale invariance; a ramus intermedius makes the left main a trifurcation and biases Delta_HK negative, the predicted risk direction, with no exclusion rule (**suspected**, prevalence unchecked); the torsion argument (small out-of-plane RMS) does not prove noise, because torsion diverges where curvature is near zero, though dropping torsion is probably right (**suspected**); and the EDT junction offset and 3 mm window have no sensitivity analysis (**suspected**).

## Correlation: prune very little, but measure reliability and shared radii first

The pairs the document lists as one family are overstated numerically. In simulation with a 5% to 10% biological deviation from the HK law, Delta_HK correlates about **-0.94 to -0.97 with F** and 0.94 to 1.00 with the area ratio, but only 0.00 to 0.02 with rho and -0.02 to 0.03 with lambda (**simulated**). This follows from the algebra: Delta_HK is F residualised on rho, so it is the one member of the F, area-ratio and Delta_HK trio worth keeping. Lambda and rho are one quantity (|lambda| = -ln rho, r about -0.99), which the design already avoids by using lambda only at the left main and rho only at side branches. The practical rule is never to enter both, and never |lambda| or lambda-squared at the left main.

The larger hazards in simulation are noise-related, with the noise sd (0.1 to 0.2 mm) assumed, not measured. Squared correlation of observed with true feature was **0.40 to 0.14 for Delta_HK**, against 0.89 to 0.67 for lambda, so Delta_HK is the least reliable feature and its weight will be attenuated toward zero, which dropping or ridge cannot fix. Because the LAD radius just past the left main is read once and used both as a left main daughter and as the D1 parent, the left main and D1 Delta_HK values correlated at **-0.34** with a shared reading versus 0.01 if independent (**simulated**). If that holds on real data, it is a pipeline property that could change when Herlev-Osterbro uses a different segmenter, which is a stronger reason for pre-specified pruning than collinearity itself. Fixes in order: measure the shared radius on a window used by one bifurcation only, centre D1 Delta_HK on the left main value, or report the block's generalised VIF.

The curvature family is the one real correlation cluster and it rests on real data. Against mean curvature at the 5 mm scale, Spearman was 0.74/0.73/0.76 for 45-degree bend counts, up to 0.83 for 90-degree bends and 0.79/0.77/0.76 for maximum bend across LAD/LCx/RCA, with the other nine summaries jointly explaining R-squared of 0.86, 0.78 and 0.77 (implied VIF about 7, 4.5, 4.4). Length-scaled totals (total turning, arc-chord) correlate 0.87 to 0.90 with length. Keep exactly one curvature scalar in Model 1 and add none of the others. The literature backs the document's stance that collinearity harms weights, not predictions: collinearity did not affect predictive performance but hurt selection stability, most for lasso ([Leeuwenberg 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.01603)), and selection compromises inference ([Heinze 2018](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/bimj.201700067)). The document's own pairwise-0.7 rule sits awkwardly beside its warning against thresholds, and the likelihood-ratio tests per family are not standard chi-square under ridge (use unpenalised fits or permutation). A pre-specified outcome-blind protocol fits: freeze the candidate list; on ImageCAS-X only compute Spearman, variable clustering ([Hmisc varclus](https://rdrr.io/cran/Hmisc/man/varclus.html)), GVIF and reliability under perturbed junction offset; within a cluster above 0.7 keep the most reliable member; demote Delta_HK to exploratory if its reliability falls below a preset floor (the 0.5 value is my suggestion, not a cited threshold).

## Model choice: penalised logistic first, the CNN cannot be estimated

Model 1 is the right primary, but as ridge or Firth logistic regression with penalty chosen by nested cross-validation, and with a **clinical-only Model 0** (age, sex, dominance, plus risk factors and calcium score if the cohort has them). Without Model 0 the document cannot separate "geometry predicts plaque" from "age and sex predict plaque", and it omits the classical risk factors that dominate 10-year incidence and that set the flow term in shear (**confirmed** absent from the text). Penalisation helps at low events per variable ([Pavlou 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26514699/)) but is not a guarantee, since ridge tuning is itself noisy when events and R-squared are small ([Riley 2021](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8026952/), [Van Calster 2020](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0962280220921415)).

Sample size should follow Riley's criteria (shrinkage of at least 0.9, small optimism, precise overall risk), which scale with the number of candidate parameters ([Riley primer](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12106283/)). My own calculation from the published formula, not verified with `pmsampsize`, gives for 12 parameters roughly **5,340 participants at Cox-Snell R-squared 0.02, 2,100 at 0.05 and 1,020 at 0.10**, and for 5 parameters 2,225, 875 and 425. Geometry-only models will probably sit at the low end, so the document's 240 events (20 x 12) is likely optimistic; the cohort size and event counts are unknown until access, and no source anchors the expected R-squared. A CNN with filters and a head has far more effective parameters, so Model 2 is very likely underpowered, and a null result would be uninformative rather than negative. Machine learning showed no benefit over logistic regression across 71 clinical prediction studies ([Christodoulou 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30763612/)), and I found no published centerline-curvature-profile model for incident plaque. The design also confounds "position of bends" with "nonlinear network versus linear model", which the matched control network only partly resolves while inheriting the same estimation problem.

The better comparator is the same penalised logistic model with 3 to 5 functional-PCA scores of kappa(s), or curvature over proximal, mid and distal thirds. This is nested (likelihood-ratio testable), interpretable as bend location, and costs 3 to 5 parameters ([penalised spline functional logit](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11749-012-0307-1)). It needs a stated padding rule for arteries shorter than L. The CNN plus control stays as a labelled exploratory analysis; tree ensembles are not recommended as primaries.

Reviewer-facing design gaps, all **confirmed absent** from the document, with proposed fixes marked as reasoning rather than sourced. Resample by patient in every bootstrap and cross-validation fold, since a patient's three domains are correlated and the three domain results are not independent confirmations ([cluster bootstrap](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5965657/)); a pooled GEE or random-intercept sensitivity model with a domain-by-curvature interaction would test whether curvature acts alike across arteries. Report optimism-corrected bootstrap discrimination, calibration slope and intercept, and Brier score, following TRIPOD+AI ([PMC11025451](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11025451/)) and stating that no external validation cohort exists. The outcome is a two-scan, roughly ten-year interval, so plaque onset is interval-censored; if the interval varies, a fixed-horizon logistic is misspecified, and loss to rescan, death and MI in between select on survival (my reasoning, no source specific to serial-imaging plaque studies). The PDA/PLA reassignment by dominance makes outcome definition depend on a predictor. The header comment that the 10-year and cohort-size facts are unconfirmed contradicts the body, which states them as fact.

## Conclusion

The review changes the priority order of the document's worries. Collinearity, which the document spends most effort on, is the smallest problem: the caliber block is nearly orthogonal in simulation, the curvature family is handled by keeping one scalar, and the risk that matters is reliability and shared-reading noise, which pruning cannot fix. The exposed items are the unearned pre-specified sign and the unmeasured radius error, and both can be settled with work on ImageCAS-X before any Herlev-Osterbro outcome is opened. Every bifurcation correlation and reliability figure here should be treated as a hypothesis until the 150-case run replaces the simulation.

Unread or unchecked: Huo-Kassab 2012 itself, the Han/ICONIC hazard ratios and CIs, most other bib-note-only studies (Sakellarios, van der Giessen, Kashyap, Dodge, Veltman, Gebhard, Khan, Cui), the CGPS scan protocol, and the Riley formula text. The Ogundimu abstract conclusion was read on the PMC page, but its applicability to logistic models is my reading.
